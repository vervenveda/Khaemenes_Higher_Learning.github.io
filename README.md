## Overview

**Khaemenes Academy™ Higher Learning** is the adult education, college readiness, career development, and lifelong learning division of the Khaemenes Academy educational network.

The mission of the Higher Learning platform is to provide **free, accessible, professional-quality education** for learners of all backgrounds, whether they are returning to school, preparing for the GED, changing careers, pursuing higher education, building professional skills, or simply continuing their lifelong pursuit of knowledge.

Rather than functioning as a single course catalog, Higher Learning is designed as a continually expanding educational campus with structured learning pathways, practical resources, and comprehensive support tools.

---

# Educational Philosophy

Higher Learning is built around several guiding principles:

- Learning should be available throughout life.
- Education should remove unnecessary barriers.
- Adult learners deserve beautiful educational environments.
- Courses should be practical, organized, and immediately useful.
- Learners should own their educational records.
- Families should be able to participate when appropriate.
- Professional quality should never require expensive tuition.
