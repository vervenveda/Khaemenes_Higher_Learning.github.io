# Khaemenes Higher Learning Associate-Level Certificate

This directory defines the first two-year certificate catalog for Khaemenes Higher Learning.

## Credential boundary

The program is free and non-accredited. It is not an associate degree, does not itself award institutional college credit, and does not guarantee transfer, admission, employment, licensure, or professional scope. It is designed as structured first-two-years-of-bachelor-level preparation with transparent learning evidence.

## Program model

- 60 Khaemenes learning units
- 4 suggested terms
- 24 units shared collegiate core
- 30 units concentration study
- 6 units integrative capstone
- 80% mastery floor at required knowledge gates
- separate laboratory, practical, studio, safety, portfolio, and evaluator evidence where applicable

One Khaemenes learning unit represents approximately 15 hours of documented study/practice/evidence. Learning units are internal program measures, not transferable credits.

## Concentrations

1. Arts, Design & Creative Practice
2. Sciences & Scientific Inquiry
3. Civics, Governance & Public Life
4. Health, Psychology & Wellbeing
5. Business, Finance & Enterprise

The catalog intentionally records the maturity of source materials. A resource hall or pathway is not silently represented as a completed standalone course.
