Khaemenes Higher Learning

Associate-Level Certificate

Two years of structured, free higher learning with concentrations in Arts, Sciences, Civics, Health, and Business.

Free educational certificate · not accredited.
This program is designed as first-two-years-of-bachelor-level preparation. Its 60 Khaemenes learning units are internal measures of documented study and are not institutional college credits. Transfer, admission, professional licensure, and college-credit decisions remain with the receiving institution or authority.

Program Architecture

Every learner completes the same 24-unit collegiate core, 30 units in one concentration, and a 6-unit integrative capstone. The mastery floor is 80%, with separate practical, laboratory, studio, safety, or portfolio evidence where required.
