
#Khaemenes Mathematics 

is a continuous secondary-to-advanced mathematics program built around mastery, placement flexibility, mathematical modelling, computation, proof, research, communication, and student-owned records.

It begins by repairing foundational mathematical fluency rather than assuming it. It moves through the complete traditional secondary sequence. It reaches Calculus I before graduation for prepared learners. Higher Learning picks up directly with Calculus II, then deliberately divides into applied, computational, data, proof, structural, analytical and geometric mathematics.

And at the upper end it is no longer simply “college prep.” It is preparing a capable student for serious undergraduate mathematics and eventual graduate-level study.
