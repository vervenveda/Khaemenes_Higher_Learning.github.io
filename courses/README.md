# Khaemenes Higher Learning Mathematics

The canonical Higher Learning Mathematics Department entrance is `courses/index.html`.

## Directory contract

The public department landing page lives directly at `courses/` so the existing collegiate mathematics course folders do not need to move. Precalculus & Trigonometry and Calculus I remain under `courses/mathematics/` as migrated compatibility trees; the nested `courses/mathematics/index.html` redirects learners back to the canonical department landing page.

The mathematics catalog currently includes twelve complete entrances:

1. Precalculus & Trigonometry
2. Calculus I
3. Calculus II
4. Linear Algebra
5. Differential Equations
6. Statistics
7. Discrete Mathematics
8. Introduction to Proofs
9. Real & Complex Analysis
10. Abstract Algebra
11. Topology & Differential Geometry
12. Advanced Geometric Topology & Mathematical Physics

Other Higher Learning subjects may also have folders beneath `courses/`. Their presence does not change the canonical Mathematics Department entrance. New campus departments should be classified explicitly in the campus directory rather than inferred solely from folder location.

The mathematics program is a continuous secondary-to-advanced sequence built around mastery, placement flexibility, modelling, computation, proof, research, communication, and student-owned records.
