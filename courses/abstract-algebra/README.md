# Khaemenes Higher Learning Abstract Algebra

A rigorous advanced sequence in algebraic structures, groups, group actions, quotient theory, Sylow theory, rings, ideals, factorization, fields, finite fields, Galois theory, modules, coding, cryptography, computational algebra, and research-level exposition.

- 36 weeks · 180 sessions
- 13 units · 92 lessons · 92 faculty keys
- 13 investigations · 13 mastery checks
- 40-question diagnostic · 60-question midterm · 100-question final
- Three pathways and three interactive laboratories

**Curriculum author:** Jennifer Pearl
