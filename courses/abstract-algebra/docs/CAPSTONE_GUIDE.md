# Abstract Algebra Research Capstone

The capstone requires a theorem-centered investigation involving one or more algebraic structures.

## Required evidence

1. A feasible research question in group, ring, field, module, Galois, coding, representation, computational, or related algebra.
2. Precise definitions, notation, prerequisites, and a dependency map.
3. Verified literature and a source architecture.
4. Examples and nonexamples, including at least one meaningful finite or computational model.
5. A principal theorem sequence with complete proofs.
6. A quotient, extension, decomposition, action, or structure-preserving-map analysis.
7. Reproducible computations with algorithms, parameters, and independent checks.
8. A counterexample or hypothesis stress test.
9. Limitations, unanswered questions, and a future research direction.
10. Journal-style exposition and oral defense.

The capstone is evaluated for correctness, mathematical maturity, structural insight, proof architecture, source integrity, reproducibility, and the learner’s ability to defend every claim.
