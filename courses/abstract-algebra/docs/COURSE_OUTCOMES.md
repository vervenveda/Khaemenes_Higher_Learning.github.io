# Course Outcomes

Learners completing this course should be able to:

- Define and analyze groups, rings, fields, modules, actions, ideals, quotient structures, and extensions.
- Construct complete proofs of foundational and advanced algebraic theorems with every hypothesis verified.
- Use homomorphisms, kernels, images, quotient objects, and isomorphism theorems to classify structure.
- Apply Sylow theory, factorization theory, finite fields, Galois theory, and module decomposition.
- Connect abstract algebra to coding, cryptography, symmetry, linear algebra, number theory, and computation.
- Design, document, reproduce, and orally defend an advanced algebra research project.
