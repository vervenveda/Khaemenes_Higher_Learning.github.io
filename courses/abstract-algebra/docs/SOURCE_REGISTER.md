# Source Register

The course architecture was checked against established undergraduate and graduate-preparatory algebra sequences. All Khaemenes lessons, problems, assessments, keys, laboratories, and prose are original.

- MIT OpenCourseWare 18.703 — Modern Algebra: groups, subgroups, cosets, permutation groups, homomorphisms, quotient groups, Sylow theory, rings, ideals, domains, polynomial rings, and finite fields.
- MIT OpenCourseWare 18.701/18.702 — Algebra I and II: symmetry groups, representations, rings, ideals, fields, polynomial rings, modules, factorization, extensions, and Galois theory.
- American Mathematical Society, Abstract Algebra: An Integrated Approach — groups, rings, vector spaces, fields, Galois theory, modules, and advanced algebraic directions.
- American Mathematical Society, Algebra in Action — group actions, rings, fields, Diophantine motivation, and Galois-theoretic polynomial solvability.


## Use

Sources inform topic scope, sequencing, and academic-level calibration. No source exercises, solution text, or lecture prose were copied.
