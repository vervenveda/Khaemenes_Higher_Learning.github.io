About Abstract Algebra

Abstract Algebra studies mathematical structure through operations, symmetry, equivalence, and abstraction. Students learn to work fluently with axiomatic systems while proving results about groups, rings, fields, and related constructions.

Course Features

Groups & SymmetryGroups, subgroups, homomorphisms, quotient groups, actions, and finite structures develop algebraic symmetry.
Rings & FactorizationRings, ideals, quotient rings, polynomial rings, divisibility, and factorization broaden structural reasoning.
Fields & ExtensionsFields, finite fields, extension fields, algebraic elements, and polynomial roots connect algebraic systems.
Proof & Galois FoundationsIsomorphism theorems, structural proof, field extensions, and introductory Galois ideas prepare advanced algebra study.
