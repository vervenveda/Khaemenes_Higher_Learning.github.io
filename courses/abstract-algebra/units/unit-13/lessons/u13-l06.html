Computational Algebra and Reproducible Verification

Designing exact computations with documented algorithms, bounds, and independent checks. This lesson develops structural algebra and proof fluency, not merely symbolic manipulation. Examples and computation guide conjecture formation; only a valid argument establishes the theorem.
