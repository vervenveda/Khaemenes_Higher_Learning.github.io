# ARCHAEMENES_INTEGRATION.md

## Canonical rule
The course uses **Archaemenes — Scholar of Khaemenes Academy**.

Wise Owl, Academy Mentor, and Scholar are not separate educational AIs. They are developmental expressions of one Archaemenes identity.

## Maturation path
- Kinder Garden: concrete, playful, clue-first discovery.
- School-age / Pre-Algebra: structured examples, question-centered reasoning, adaptive pathway support.
- Higher Learning: Socratic proof guidance, theorem dependency, counterexample search, reproducibility, literature awareness, and oral defense.

## NAIB vs Archaemenes
Recommended separation of responsibilities:
- **NAIB/resource layer:** finds and routes relevant registered tools/resources from lesson context.
- **Archaemenes/educational layer:** decides how to teach, what clue to offer, what evidence is missing, and how much independence to expect.
- **Learner record layer:** preserves progress, pathway, scores, proof/lab evidence, reflection, recurring questions, and learner preferences.

## Static-site honesty
This package exposes structured context for Archaemenes but does not pretend that a local JavaScript fallback is a remote generative AI service.
