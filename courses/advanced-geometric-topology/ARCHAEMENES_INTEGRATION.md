## Public instructional rule

This course uses **Archaemenes — Scholar of Khaemenes Academy** as its educational mentor expression.

Wise Owl, Academy Mentor, and Scholar are developmental expressions of one Archaemenes identity across the Khaemenes learning continuum.

## Mathematics Continuum

Advanced Geometric Topology is registered as:

- Course ID: `KH-HL-AGTGMP`
- Mathematics consumer: `khaemenes.math.consumer.001`
- Shared continuum: `khaemenes.mathematics.continuum`

The course remains authoritative for its own lessons, assessments, sequence, evidence requirements, and local learner records. The shared Mathematics Continuum supplies common mathematics metadata, evidence/proof language, approved public tool identities, progression strands, and bounded Mathematics Resource Cloud capabilities.

## Instructional progression

- Early learning: concrete, playful, clue-first discovery.
- School-age mathematics: structured examples, question-centered reasoning, representation, and adaptive pathway support.
- Higher Learning: Socratic proof guidance, theorem dependency, counterexample search, reproducibility, literature awareness, and oral defense.

## Public learning roles

- **Archaemenes:** instructional guidance, clues, mathematical questioning, and evidence-aware teaching.
- **Mathematics Continuum:** shared public mathematics vocabulary, progression, tool metadata, and evidence guardrails.
- **Course engine:** current lesson, pathway, local progress, reflections, and exportable learner record.
- **Public resources:** approved calculators, CAD/geometry tools, evidence tools, and reviewed lab resources matched to lesson context.

## Static-site honesty

The browser course can use local rule-based guidance and shared public metadata. It does not claim that a visualization proves a theorem, that an objective score certifies proof mastery, or that a local fallback is a remote AI service.

## Graceful fallback

If the Mathematics Continuum cannot be loaded, the course continues in local mode without losing lesson content or learner progress.
