Assessment Architecture

Unit mastery

Concept/objective check ≥80%, written theorem/proof evidence, explicit computation, example/counterexample, lab interpretation where applicable, and correction of significant errors.

Mastery status

Objective scores never certify proof mastery by themselves. Browser scoring may establish conceptual readiness; mathematical proof and lab evidence remain pending review until explicitly verified.

Midterm · after Week 18

20% conceptual · 30% proof · 20% computation · 15% lab interpretation · 15% synthesis/counterexample.

Final

20% conceptual · 35% written proofs · 20% computation · 10% lab analysis · 15% cross-domain synthesis.

Capstone & oral defense

Research question, definitions, source register, theorem dependency map, complete proof sequence, examples/nonexamples, reproducible computation where appropriate, limitations, revision history, oral defense, and correction cycle.
