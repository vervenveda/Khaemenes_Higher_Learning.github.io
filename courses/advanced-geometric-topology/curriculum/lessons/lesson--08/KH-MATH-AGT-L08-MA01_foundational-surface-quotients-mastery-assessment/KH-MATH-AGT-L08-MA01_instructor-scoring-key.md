## Section II — verification

### Problem 4
Word: a b c a^-1 b^-1 c^-1.
Using vertices v0...v5 and the course convention:
- a: v0~v4, v1~v3
- b: v1~v5, v2~v4
- c: v2~v0, v3~v5
These relations yield two vertex classes: {v0,v2,v4} and {v1,v3,v5}.
Thus V=2, E=3, F=1 and chi=0.
Do not award a classification name merely from chi.

### Problem 5
Word: a b a^-1 b.
- a: v0~v3, v1~v2
- b (same exponent sign): v1~v3 and v2~v0
Together all four vertices form one class.
Thus V=1, E=2, F=1 and chi=0.
The repeated b with the same exponent sign supplies nonorientable-pattern evidence under the course convention.
A name is not required.

### Problem 6
Both examples have chi=0 but have different vertex-class structures (V=2 versus V=1), different E counts (3 versus 2), and different orientation patterns. Equal chi alone is insufficient for homeomorphism.
