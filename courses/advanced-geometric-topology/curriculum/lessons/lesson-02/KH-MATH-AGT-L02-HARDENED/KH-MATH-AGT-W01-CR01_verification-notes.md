# Lesson 02 — Verification Notes

## Scope
Reusable concept reference for quotient spaces and polygonal schemas.

## Mathematical verification targets
- Quotient topology via inverse images under the natural surjection q:X→X/~.
- Equivalence-class construction X/~.
- Surface convention: Hausdorff, second-countable, locally homeomorphic to R².
- Standard torus as a quotient under opposite-edge identifications.
- Polygonal presentations as a route from triangulated compact connected surfaces to paired-edge models.
- Separation of edge-orientation notation from global orientability.
- Euler characteristic uses quotient-cell counts, not raw polygon counts.
