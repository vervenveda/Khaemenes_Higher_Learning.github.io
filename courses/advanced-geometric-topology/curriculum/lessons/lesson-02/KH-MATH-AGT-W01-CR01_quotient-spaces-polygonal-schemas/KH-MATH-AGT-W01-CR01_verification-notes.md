# KH-MATH-AGT-W01-CR01 — Verification Notes

## Scope
Reusable concept reference for quotient spaces and polygonal schemas.

## Mathematical verification targets
- Quotient topology definition via inverse images under a surjective quotient map.
- Equivalence-class construction X/~.
- Surface as a 2-manifold with standard separation/countability assumptions.
- Standard torus as a quotient of I×I by opposite-edge identifications.
- Polygonal models as a rigorous route from triangulated compact connected surfaces to paired-edge polygons.
- Separation of edge-orientation notation from global orientability.
- Euler characteristic uses quotient cell counts, not raw polygon counts.

## Editorial evidence
Cornell point-set topology notes give a direct definition of quotient topology and treat surfaces as quotient-space examples.
University of Chicago surface-classification notes define surfaces and explicitly describe the torus as a quotient of I×I.
Massey's surface-classification material gives a rigorous construction of a polygon with sides identified in pairs from a triangulated connected surface.

## Publication rule
These sources verify the mathematics. They are not required student readings. The student-facing reference remains internally complete and original.
