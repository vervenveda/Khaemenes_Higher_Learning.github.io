# LAB01 Verification Notes

## Curriculum role
This laboratory is the fifth instructional day of Week 1. It integrates the reading, reference, worked examples, and proof workshop into reproducible mathematical investigation.

## Mathematical safeguards
- Quotient cell counts are obtained after identifications.
- Euler characteristic is treated as an invariant, not a complete classifier.
- Orientability evidence is kept distinct from edge-arrow notation.
- The local Euclidean condition is required when working outside established proper paired-edge surface constructions.
- The full surface-classification theorem is not assumed.
- Physical folding and visualization are explicitly classified as exploratory evidence rather than proof.

## A+++ laboratory standard
A completed lab must preserve:
1. inputs and conventions;
2. endpoint relations;
3. equivalence-class reasoning;
4. computations;
5. status of each conclusion;
6. error/correction record;
7. reproducibility.

External sources are editorial verification only. There is no required external learner dependency.
