# Lesson 06 / PS01 Verification Notes

## Structural change
The course is self-paced. This resource therefore uses `L06` as its instructional position rather than treating the material as a sixth day inside a calendar week.

Legacy companion IDs from Lessons 01–05 retain their existing `W01` identifiers to avoid breaking already-uploaded resources. Their IDs can be normalized later only through an intentional migration.

## Assessment design
30 problems progress through:
1. foundations;
2. endpoint and vertex audits;
3. invariant logic;
4. higher-complexity presentations;
5. proof and local forensics;
6. synthesis.

The set does not introduce the full classification theorem before it has been taught.

## Mastery rule
Recommended threshold: 80%, but numerical score alone is insufficient when a learner has an unresolved major misconception in quotient identification or proof logic.

## Independence
No paid text, external site, calculator, or physical model is required.
