## Assessment hardening
No solutions or hidden answer keys are embedded in the student page. Each problem has printable response space. The capstone receives a dedicated long-form page. Parts begin on fresh pages in print. Student identification and scoring fields are included.

## Mathematical precision
The original statement in Problem 13 is retained: homeomorphic finite-CW surfaces have equal Euler characteristic; students are asked only for its contrapositive. Equal χ is never treated as sufficient for homeomorphism.

## External verification
A separate graduate geometry-processing course likewise treats Euler characteristic as a topological invariant computed from V-E+F for an appropriate surface mesh. This was used only as an editorial cross-check, not as a learner dependency.
