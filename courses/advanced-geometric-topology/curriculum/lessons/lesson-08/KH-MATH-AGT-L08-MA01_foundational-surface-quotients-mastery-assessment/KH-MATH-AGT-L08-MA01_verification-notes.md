# L08-MA01 Verification Notes

## Assessment intent
Lesson 08 is a mastery gate for the foundational quotient-surface sequence. It tests transfer rather than recall by using new prompts and unfamiliar combinations.

## Internal verification
The two unseen fixed polygonal audits were independently checked from endpoint relations:
- `a b c a^-1 b^-1 c^-1` -> V=2, E=3, F=1, chi=0.
- `a b a^-1 b` -> V=1, E=2, F=1, chi=0.

The assessment deliberately demonstrates that equal Euler characteristic need not imply identical quotient data or homeomorphism.

## Safeguards
- Full surface classification is not assumed.
- Surface naming is not required for unfamiliar schemes.
- Quotient topology and open-map logic are distinguished.
- Local Euclidean verification is explicitly assessed.
- 80% alone cannot conceal a critical conceptual error.
- Reassessment is allowed in the self-paced model.
