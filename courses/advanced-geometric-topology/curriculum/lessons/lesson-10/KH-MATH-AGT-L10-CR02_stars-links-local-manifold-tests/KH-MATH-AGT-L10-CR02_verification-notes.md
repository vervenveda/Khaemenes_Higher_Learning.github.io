# Lesson 10 Verification Notes

## Mathematical frame
The lesson uses standard star/link constructions in a finite simplicial complex.

For a triangulated 2-manifold:
- the link of an interior vertex is a circle;
- the link of a boundary vertex is an interval;
- an interior edge has two incident triangles / two-point link;
- a boundary edge has one incident triangle / one-point link;
- branching or disconnected links diagnose nonmanifold behavior.

## Important scope discipline
The lesson separates local manifold criteria from global properties such as connectedness.

It also treats purity and edge-incidence checks as necessary diagnostics but not, by themselves, sufficient substitutes for vertex-link analysis.

## Verification sources
Primary/academic references on abstract simplicial complexes and geometric realization were used to verify terminology and the combinatorial setting. The manifold-link criteria are standard PL/combinatorial-manifold facts; external references are editorial verification and are not learner dependencies.
