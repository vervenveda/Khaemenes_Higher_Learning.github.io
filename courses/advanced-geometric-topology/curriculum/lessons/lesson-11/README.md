# Lesson 11 — Subdivision & Triangulation Invariance

**Resource:** `KH-MATH-AGT-L11-R03`  
**Arc:** 02 — Triangulations & Combinatorial Surfaces  
**Delivery:** Self-Paced

Introduces subdivision, barycentric subdivision, face-chain structure, iterated refinement, Euler-characteristic invariance, local link preservation, and the distinction between changing a triangulation and changing the underlying topological space.

The lesson deliberately avoids overclaiming that arbitrary homeomorphic triangulations are simplicially isomorphic or automatically related by a theorem not yet established.
