# Lesson 12 — Simplicial Maps & Combinatorial Topology

**Resource:** `KH-MATH-AGT-L12-R04`  
**Arc:** 02 — Triangulations & Combinatorial Surfaces  
**Delivery:** Self-Paced

Moves Arc 02 from simplicial spaces to maps between them. Learners define and audit simplicial maps, construct their geometric realizations, study dimension collapse and simplicial isomorphisms, and encounter the simplicial approximation theorem as the bridge from continuous maps to combinatorial topology.

The independent Map Laboratory requires learners to construct a valid injective map, a valid dimension-collapsing map, and an invalid vertex rule, then audit all three.
