# KH-MATH-AGT-W01-PW01 — Mathematical Verification Notes

## Verification scope

This proof workshop was checked against rigorous treatments of polygonal quotients and surface classification.

### Paired-edge quotient theorem
University of Chicago surface-classification notes state that a polygonal region with an even number of edges, paired by simplicial isomorphisms, yields a compact surface. The proof is organized by face points, edge points, and vertex points. That architecture is reflected in Workshop E.

### Polygonal reduction
Cornell topology notes state that every compact connected triangulable surface is homeomorphic to a quotient obtained from a polygonal region by identifying edges in pairs. This supports the course's use of polygonal presentations while keeping the full classification theorem out of Week 1.

### Proof discipline
The workshop deliberately separates:
- equivalence-class arguments;
- quotient cell counts;
- invariant calculations;
- local surface arguments;
- classification conclusions.

The course does not treat Euler characteristic alone as a complete classifier. The torus/Klein-bottle counterexample is used explicitly to block that false converse.
