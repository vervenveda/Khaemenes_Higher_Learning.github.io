# WE01 Verification Notes

The examples were checked against rigorous surface-presentation conventions before publication.

## Benchmark words used
- Sphere: `aa^-1` (and the equivalent four-edge presentation `abb^-1a^-1`)
- Torus: `aba^-1b^-1`
- Real projective plane: `aa`
- Klein bottle: `abab^-1`

These agree with the polygonal-presentation conventions used in the University of Chicago surface-classification reference.

## Pedagogical safeguards
- Euler characteristic is never treated as a complete classifier by itself.
- The torus/Klein-bottle comparison explicitly demonstrates equal Euler characteristic with different orientability.
- The local Euclidean condition is retained as a required check outside the paired-edge theorem's hypotheses.
- Full classification is not assumed before the course establishes it.
- External sources verify the mathematics but are not learner dependencies.
