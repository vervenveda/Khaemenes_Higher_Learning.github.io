# W01D2 Teacher / Reviewer Key

## Core mathematical checkpoints
- Standard torus word: `aba^-1b^-1`.
- Quotient CW counts: `V=1`, `E=2`, `F=1`, hence `chi=0`.
- Torus and Klein bottle both have `chi=0`.
- They are not homeomorphic because orientability is a homeomorphism invariant and differs between them.
- Equal Euler characteristic is necessary for homeomorphism of finite CW complexes, not sufficient in general.

## Primary proof rubric
A strong response explicitly labels vertices, derives endpoint identifications from both edge pairings, takes transitive closure, counts quotient edge classes and the 2-cell, then computes Euler characteristic. Do not accept 'the picture shows it' as the entire vertex argument.

## Counterexample proof
The learner must establish:
1. torus has `chi=0`;
2. Klein bottle has `chi=0`;
3. torus is orientable;
4. Klein bottle is nonorientable;
5. orientability is invariant under homeomorphism;
6. therefore equal `chi` does not imply homeomorphism.
