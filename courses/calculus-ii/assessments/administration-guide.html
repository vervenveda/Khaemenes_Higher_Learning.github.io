Faculty protocol

Assessment Administration

Use transparent, repeatable procedures that protect integrity while preserving accessibility and meaningful reassessment.

Before

  • Verify learner identity or local label.
  • State calculator and reference permissions.
  • Provide approved accommodations.
  • Prepare paper for derivations and diagrams.

During

  • Maintain a quiet, interruption-minimized setting.
  • Record start and completion times.
  • Do not coach content during a formal attempt.
  • Preserve written work with the score record.

After

  • Review explanations and written reasoning.
  • Require corrections for every missed concept.
  • Use a fresh randomized attempt for reassessment.
  • Archive first, corrected, and best evidence.

Recommended weighting

Weekly coursework 25% · Unit mastery 20% · Investigations 20% · Midterm 10% · Final 15% · Capstone and oral defense 10%.
