About Calculus II

Calculus II extends the ideas of differentiation and integration into more advanced techniques, infinite processes, parametric relationships, polar models, and cumulative reasoning. The course emphasizes method selection, interpretation, and mathematical justification.

Course Features

Advanced IntegrationStudents use substitution, integration by parts, partial fractions, improper integrals, and strategic method selection.
Sequences & SeriesConvergence, power series, Taylor and Maclaurin methods, and approximation develop reasoning about infinite processes.
Parametric & PolarParametric curves, polar coordinates, arc length, area, and related calculus broaden representation and modelling.
ApplicationsDifferential equations, physical models, accumulated change, and approximation connect techniques to applied problems.
