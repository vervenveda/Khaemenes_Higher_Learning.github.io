Applied collegiate investigation

Rational Decomposition & Verification Studio

How does algebraic decomposition expose the logarithmic, inverse-trigonometric, and rational pieces hidden inside a rational function?

Investigation brief

Design or analyze a model involving transfer functions, rates, probability models, and rational response curves. Define a question that requires the unit’s mathematics, identify data or defensible assumptions, derive the model, compute or approximate a result, validate it independently, and state limitations.

Required submission

  1. Question, context, diagram, variables, units, and assumptions.
  2. Mathematical derivation with theorem or method justification.
  3. Exact and/or numerical result with technology record.
  4. Error, sensitivity, residual, or convergence evidence.
  5. Interpretation, limitations, and next research question.

Oral defense prompts

  • Why is this method valid?
  • What alternative did you reject?
  • How did you verify the result?
  • Which assumption matters most?
  • How would the model change?
