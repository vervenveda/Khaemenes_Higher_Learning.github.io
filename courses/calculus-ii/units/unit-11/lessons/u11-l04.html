Concept development

Build the structure before choosing the procedure.

Geometric & Telescoping Series belongs to the broader Calculus II practice of classifying structure before computation. A complete solution addresses hypotheses, domain, exact notation, units, interpretation, and verification.

Worked examples

Follow the reasoning one defensible step at a time.

Worked reasoning A

Problem: Find Σ_{n=0}∞3(1/4)^n.

Solution: 4.

Worked reasoning B

Problem: Determine Σ_{n=1}∞ln((n+1)/n).

Solution: It diverges because partial sums equal ln(N+1).
