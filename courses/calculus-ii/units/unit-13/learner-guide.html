Power Series, Taylor Series & Modelling Capstone

How do power series represent functions, control approximation error, solve problems, and bridge single-variable calculus to advanced analysis?

Prerequisite readiness

Review vocabulary, algebraic and trigonometric structure, Calculus I relationships, notation, and units before beginning. Use the Foundation pathway whenever retrieval evidence is incomplete.

Weekly evidence

Each week should produce annotated notes, complete derivations, corrected problems, one application, one verification artifact, and a short reflection.

Study rhythm

  1. Retrieve prerequisite ideas.
  2. Study the lesson and rebuild examples.
  3. Complete assigned pathway work.
  4. Conduct error analysis and application.
  5. Correct and archive evidence.

Mastery rule

Target 80% with complete reasoning. Reassessment follows analysis of missed work, reteaching, and a fresh attempt.
