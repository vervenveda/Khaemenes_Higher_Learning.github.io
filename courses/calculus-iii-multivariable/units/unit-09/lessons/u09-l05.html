Calculus III · Unit 09 · Lesson 05

Piecewise Boundaries and Regions with Holes

managing composite and multiply connected boundaries.

Learning target

Explain, compute, interpret and verify managing composite and multiply connected boundaries using exact notation, geometric reasoning and appropriate units.

∮_C P dx+Q dy=∬_D(Q_x−P_y)dA

Concept development

How does Green's Theorem connect circulation or flux around a boundary with derivatives throughout a planar region?

  1. Identify the objects, variables, domain and coordinate system.
  2. Translate the geometric or physical statement into multivariable notation.
  3. Carry out the derivative, integral, vector or coordinate reasoning with each transformation justified.
  4. Interpret the result geometrically and check dimensions, orientation, bounds or limiting behavior.

Worked-example frame

1 · Represent. Draw or describe the relevant curve, surface, vector field, region or level set.
2 · Choose. State why the selected coordinates, derivative, integral or theorem fit the hypotheses.
3 · Compute. Show the multivariable steps without skipping bounds, vector components or Jacobian factors.
4 · Verify. Check the result by geometry, units, limiting cases, an alternate method or numerical evidence.
