Graphs, Cylinders, Spheres and Common Surface Parameterizations

choosing efficient parameterizations for standard geometries.

Learning target

Explain, compute, interpret and verify choosing efficient parameterizations for standard geometries using exact notation, geometric reasoning and appropriate units.

Concept development

How do parameterizations encode surfaces and how do surface integrals accumulate scalar and vector quantities?
