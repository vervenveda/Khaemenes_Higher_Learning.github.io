Four-week mastery record

The four weekly records are read from this browser. A completed week requires a scored 80% or higher attempt, five written responses of at least 60 characters each, and a reviewer-confirmed review. This is a local, self-reported educational record and not an identity-verified credential.

Cumulative final examination

40 questions drawn as 10 from each week's 20-question bank, without replacement within that week. One answer per question; 80% is required. Answers and explanations appear after a response. Complete all four weeks before taking the final.

Capstone review

Review the Week 4 moot court case brief for accurate amendment citation, case sources, legal reasoning and counterargument. The reviewer must record an approval after examining the written work. This approval is not independently verified.
