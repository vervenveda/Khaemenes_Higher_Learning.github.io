Week 1 · Foundations of the Bill of Rights

Five original daily readings, historical evidence assignments, fifteen vocabulary terms, primary-source links, a randomized ten-question weekly assessment drawn from twenty questions, 80% objective mastery, printable work, and student evidence export/import.

Week 2 · Amendments I–III

Five new daily readings on religion, expression, the right to keep and bear arms, military quartering, and evidence-based constitutional analysis. Includes vocabulary, written work, original legal sources, print and JSON portfolio support, and a twenty-question randomized assessment bank.

Week 3 · Amendments IV–VIII

Five daily constitutional justice lessons covering searches, due process, criminal trials, civil juries, and excessive bail, fines, and punishment. Original readings, case-based assignments, vocabulary, primary sources, written-work review, and randomized mastery assessment.

Open Week 3 · Justice and Legal Protection →

Week 4 · Amendments IX–X and Constitutional Synthesis

Five lessons on unenumerated rights, reserved powers, Fourteenth Amendment incorporation, constitutional case research, and an original final moot-court exercise. Weekly mastery is separate from eventual full-course certification.

Educator and family note

This gateway introduces constitutional ideas; it does not replace the existing Grade 10–11 courses. Work here is formative practice, not verified 80% mastery. Full lessons, accessible printable packets, randomized assessment banks, evidence portfolios, and mastery-gated progression are planned as separate audited additions. Educational content is not legal advice.
