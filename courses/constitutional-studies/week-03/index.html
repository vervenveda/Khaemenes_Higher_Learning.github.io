Weekly assessment

A ten-question random sample without replacement is drawn from 20 reviewed questions. Every selection is scored only once. A new attempt starts a new shuffle; completed attempts retain their own score.

Primary-source research library

    Original Academy readings are included on this page. External sources require internet access. Interpretive legal questions should be checked against up-to-date court materials.

    Evidence-based writing rubric

    Review each daily response using the same four criteria. Each is scored 0–2 for a total of 8 points per response.

    1. Claim: clear and historically accurate position (0–2).
    2. Primary-source evidence: specific support with an identifiable citation (0–2).
    3. Historical context: dates, people, and accurate text-versus-interpretation distinctions (0–2).
    4. Reasoning: connects evidence to a claim and acknowledges a limitation or alternative (0–2).

    Suggested proficiency: at least 6/8 with no zero in source evidence. This is a reviewer-guided writing standard, not an automatically verified score.

    Teacher / parent review

    Confirm that all five written responses use evidence, distinguish text from interpretation, and discuss a competing historical argument. Objective quiz mastery alone does not verify the written work.
