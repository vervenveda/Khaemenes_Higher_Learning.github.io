# Differential Equations Capstone Guide

The capstone must use a substantive ordinary differential equation or coupled system and include: a precise physics, engineering, scientific, or social-system question; a labelled model diagram; variables, parameters, units, and conditions; derivation from rate balances or constitutive laws; analytical or numerical method justification; data or parameter statement; verification; sensitivity and uncertainty analysis; limitations and responsible-use considerations; a written technical memorandum; reproducible artifacts; and a 6–10 minute oral defense. Suitable domains include vibration, thermal systems, fluids, circuits, population or disease models, chemical kinetics, control, environmental systems, or an original theoretical/numerical study.
