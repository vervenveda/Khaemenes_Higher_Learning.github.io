# Course Outcomes

- Rate-law modelling, classification, solution verification, and qualitative analysis
- Separable, linear, exact, Bernoulli, and transformable first-order equations
- Cooling, mixing, drag, population, kinetics, and circuit models
- Euler, Heun, RK4, adaptive methods, error, stability, stiffness, and reproducibility
- Second-order linear equations, oscillation, damping, forcing, resonance, and RLC circuits
- Laplace transforms, switching, impulses, convolution, transfer functions, and poles
- Linear systems, matrix exponentials, eigenmodes, and phase portraits
- Nonlinear systems, nullclines, linearization, Lyapunov ideas, bifurcations, and limit cycles
- Power series, Frobenius methods, special functions, BVPs, Fourier methods, and PDE bridge
- Parameter estimation, uncertainty, control interpretation, capstone modelling, and oral defense
