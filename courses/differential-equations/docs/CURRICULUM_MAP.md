# Differential Equations 36-Week Curriculum Map

## Unit 01 — Differential Equations, Models & Qualitative Structure (Weeks 1–3)

**Essential question:** How do rates of change become mathematical models, and how can solutions be checked before they are solved exactly?

**Investigation:** Dynamic-System Modelling & Validation Portfolio

- Week 1: Rates of Change and Differential-Equation Models; Classification: Order, Linearity and Autonomy; Solutions, Families and Initial-Value Problems
- Week 2: Direction Fields and Isoclines; Existence, Uniqueness and Maximal Intervals
- Week 3: Autonomous Equations, Equilibria and Stability; Units, Scaling, Assumptions and Model Validation
## Unit 02 — Separable Equations, Growth, Decay & Nonlinear First-Order Models (Weeks 4–6)

**Essential question:** How does separation expose accumulation laws, thresholds, and long-term behavior in nonlinear first-order models?

**Investigation:** Growth, Decay & Threshold Modelling Laboratory

- Week 4: Separation of Variables; Exponential Growth and Decay; Doubling Time, Half-Life and Parameter Identification
- Week 5: Logistic Growth and Carrying Capacity; Threshold, Harvesting and Finite-Time Blow-Up
- Week 6: Homogeneous First-Order Substitution; Orthogonal Trajectories and Family Geometry
## Unit 03 — First-Order Linear, Exact & Transformable Equations (Weeks 7–9)

**Essential question:** How do integrating factors, potential functions, and substitutions convert structured first-order equations into solvable forms?

**Investigation:** First-Order Method Selection & Verification Atlas

- Week 7: First-Order Linear Equations and Integrating Factors; Exact Equations and Potential Functions; Integrating Factors for Nonexact Equations
- Week 8: Bernoulli Equations; Substitutions for Shifted and Composite Slopes
- Week 9: Singular Solutions and Envelopes; First-Order Strategy and Interval Audit
## Unit 04 — Applied First-Order Models in Physics, Engineering & Life Science (Weeks 10–12)

**Essential question:** How do conservation laws and constitutive relations create useful first-order models, and how are assumptions tested?

**Investigation:** Applied Rate-Law Engineering Memorandum

- Week 10: Newton Cooling and Heating; Mixing Tanks and Conservation of Solute; Falling Bodies with Linear Drag
- Week 11: Population Harvesting and Threshold Management; Chemical Kinetics and Compartment Models
- Week 12: RC Circuits and Electrical Time Constants; Assumptions, Calibration and Sensitivity in Applied Models
## Unit 05 — Numerical Methods, Error & Computational Reliability (Weeks 13–14)

**Essential question:** How do numerical solvers approximate differential equations, and how can accuracy, stability, and reproducibility be defended?

**Investigation:** Numerical Solver Comparison & Error Laboratory

- Week 13: Euler’s Method from Tangent-Line Prediction; Improved Euler and Predictor–Corrector Reasoning; Classical Fourth-Order Runge–Kutta; Local Truncation, Global Error and Convergence
- Week 14: Absolute Stability and Stiff Equations; Adaptive Step Size and Event Detection; Reproducible Numerical Experiments
## Unit 06 — Second-Order Linear Homogeneous Equations & Mechanical Response (Weeks 15–17)

**Essential question:** How do characteristic roots determine transient behavior, oscillation, and the dimension of a second-order solution space?

**Investigation:** Second-Order Response Classification Portfolio

- Week 15: Second-Order Linear Structure and Characteristic Equations; Distinct Real Roots and Exponential Modes; Repeated Roots and Generalized Modes
- Week 16: Complex Roots and Oscillatory Motion; Initial Conditions, Amplitude and Phase
- Week 17: Wronskians and Fundamental Solution Sets; Reduction of Order and Operator Verification
## Unit 07 — Forced Equations, Oscillation, Resonance & Circuit Models (Weeks 18–20)

**Essential question:** How do external inputs, damping, natural frequencies, and system parameters shape transient and steady-state response?

**Investigation:** Oscillation, Resonance & Frequency-Response Laboratory

- Week 18: Undetermined Coefficients for Standard Inputs; Variation of Parameters; Mass–Spring Models and Natural Frequency
- Week 19: Damping Regimes and Transient Design; Sinusoidal Forcing and Resonance
- Week 20: RLC Circuits as Second-Order Systems; Frequency Response, Energy and Engineering Limits
## Unit 08 — Laplace Transforms, Discontinuous Inputs & Transfer Functions (Weeks 21–23)

**Essential question:** How do transforms encode initial conditions, switching, impulses, convolution, and input–output behavior?

**Investigation:** Laplace, Impulse & Transfer-Function Analysis Portfolio

- Week 21: Laplace Transform Definition and Basic Properties; Inverse Transforms and Partial Fractions; Transforms of Derivatives and Initial-Value Problems
- Week 22: Unit-Step Functions and Piecewise Inputs; Impulse Inputs and Jump Conditions
- Week 23: Convolution and Impulse Response; Transfer Functions, Poles and Stability
## Unit 09 — Systems of Differential Equations & Matrix Methods (Weeks 24–26)

**Essential question:** How do state vectors, eigenstructure, and matrix exponentials organize coupled dynamic systems?

**Investigation:** Coupled-System State-Space Laboratory

- Week 24: From Higher-Order Equations to First-Order Systems; Linear Systems and Eigenmode Solutions; Distinct Real Eigenvalues and Phase Portraits
- Week 25: Complex Eigenvalues, Spirals and Centers; Repeated Eigenvalues and Defective Systems
- Week 26: Fundamental Matrices and Matrix Exponentials; Forced Linear Systems and Variation of Constants
## Unit 10 — Nonlinear Systems, Phase Planes & Stability (Weeks 27–29)

**Essential question:** How do nullclines, linearization, energy ideas, and bifurcations reveal nonlinear dynamics beyond explicit formulas?

**Investigation:** Nonlinear Phase-Plane & Stability Investigation

- Week 27: Phase Planes, Nullclines and Direction Fields; Jacobian Linearization Near Equilibria; Competing Species and Invariant Regions
- Week 28: Predator–Prey Models and Conserved Orbits; Nonlinear Oscillators and Energy Landscapes
- Week 29: Lyapunov Functions and Stability Certification; Bifurcations, Limit Cycles and Chaos Preview
## Unit 11 — Power-Series Solutions, Frobenius Methods & Special Functions (Weeks 30–31)

**Essential question:** How do local series expansions solve variable-coefficient equations when elementary formulas are unavailable?

**Investigation:** Series-Solution Recurrence & Error Portfolio

- Week 30: Power-Series Method at an Ordinary Point; Recurrences for Second-Order Equations; Variable Coefficients and Radius of Convergence; Frobenius Method and Indicial Equations
- Week 31: Euler–Cauchy Equations; Bessel and Legendre Foundations; Series Truncation, Numerical Comparison and Verification
## Unit 12 — Boundary-Value Problems, Fourier Methods & PDE Bridge (Weeks 32–34)

**Essential question:** How do boundary conditions select discrete modes, and how do ordinary differential equations support heat, wave, and steady-state field models?

**Investigation:** Boundary Modes, Fourier Synthesis & PDE Bridge Portfolio

- Week 32: Boundary-Value Problems versus Initial-Value Problems; Eigenvalue Boundary Problems and Discrete Modes; Sturm–Liouville Structure and Orthogonality
- Week 33: Fourier Series and Periodic Representation; Heat Equation by Separation of Variables
- Week 34: Wave Equation and Modal Oscillation; Laplace Equation and Numerical PDE Bridge
## Unit 13 — Engineering Modelling, Parameter Estimation, Control & Capstone (Weeks 35–36)

**Essential question:** How can a differential-equation model become an auditable engineering argument supported by calibration, uncertainty, computation, and oral defense?

**Investigation:** Differential Equations Engineering Capstone & Oral Defense

- Week 35: Parameter Estimation from Dynamic Data; Nondimensionalization and Similarity; Sensitivity, Identifiability and Uncertainty; Solver Selection and Reproducible Simulation
- Week 36: Feedback, Stability and Control Interpretation; Capstone Question, Model and Evidence Plan; Capstone Computation, Verification and Technical Report; Oral Defense and Advanced Differential-Equations Bridge
