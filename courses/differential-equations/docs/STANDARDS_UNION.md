# Standards and Curriculum Union

The course is an original Khaemenes Higher Learning curriculum informed by the broad topic union of established undergraduate ordinary differential equations programs. Its scope includes analytical, graphical, numerical, transform, systems, qualitative, and applied approaches, with substantial physics and engineering modelling.

The course deliberately unites five modes of knowing:

1. **Modelling:** states, flows, constitutive laws, parameters, initial/boundary data, units, and assumptions.
2. **Analytical:** exact methods, transforms, series, operator structure, and theorem hypotheses.
3. **Qualitative:** direction fields, phase lines, equilibria, stability, phase portraits, and bifurcations.
4. **Numerical:** solver selection, error, stability, stiffness, refinement, events, and reproducibility.
5. **Applied and communicative:** physics, engineering, data calibration, uncertainty, limitations, technical reporting, and oral defense.

No official affiliation, accreditation, or institutional endorsement is claimed.
