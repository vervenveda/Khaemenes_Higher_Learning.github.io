About Differential Equations

Differential Equations studies mathematical models defined by rates of change. Students analyze qualitative behavior, solve important equation families, interpret solutions, and connect differential models to physical, biological, engineering, and dynamic systems.

Course Features

First-Order ModelsSeparable, linear, autonomous, and applied first-order equations build core solution and interpretation skills.
Second-Order SystemsOscillation, forcing, resonance, characteristic methods, and mechanical models extend differential reasoning.
Transforms & SystemsLaplace transforms, systems of equations, phase behavior, and matrix methods support more complex models.
Numerical & Applied ModellingApproximation, stability, computational methods, and real-world modelling connect theory to dynamic systems.
