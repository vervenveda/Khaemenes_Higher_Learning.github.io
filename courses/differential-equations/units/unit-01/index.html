Unit lessons

Week 1

01. Rates of Change and Differential-Equation Models

Translating verbal rate laws into dependent and independent variables, parameters, and equations.

Week 1

02. Classification: Order, Linearity and Autonomy

Classifying equations by highest derivative, linearity, and explicit dependence on the independent variable.

Week 1

03. Solutions, Families and Initial-Value Problems

Distinguishing general, particular, implicit, and singular solutions and applying initial conditions.

Week 2

04. Direction Fields and Isoclines

Reading local slopes and sketching solution behavior without an explicit formula.

Week 2

05. Existence, Uniqueness and Maximal Intervals

Using continuity and local lipschitz conditions to judge whether an ivp has one local solution.

Week 3

06. Autonomous Equations, Equilibria and Stability

Using sign charts and phase lines to classify long-term behavior.

Week 3

07. Units, Scaling, Assumptions and Model Validation

Checking dimensional consistency, plausible limits, and the meaning of parameters.
