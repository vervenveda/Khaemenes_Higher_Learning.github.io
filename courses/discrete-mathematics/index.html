About Discrete Mathematics

Discrete Mathematics develops the mathematical structures and reasoning methods used throughout computer science, logic, combinatorics, networks, and algorithmic problem solving. Precision and proof are central throughout the course.

Course Features

Logic & SetsPropositions, quantifiers, sets, relations, functions, and formal reasoning establish the language of discrete mathematics.
Counting & ProbabilityCombinatorics, inclusion-exclusion, recurrence, and discrete probability develop systematic counting strategies.
Graphs & NetworksGraphs, trees, connectivity, paths, coloring, and network models connect theory with computational applications.
Algorithms & ProofInduction, invariants, recursion, complexity, and algorithmic reasoning link mathematical proof with computation.
