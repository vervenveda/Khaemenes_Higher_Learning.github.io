Mathematical Pathways

Different entrances into deeper mathematics.

The course catalog is not a rigid ladder. Students can combine applied, data, proof, structural, and geometric study while preserving a coherent advanced mathematics record.

01

Continuous & Applied

Calculus II, differential equations, linear algebra, numerical reasoning, physical modeling, engineering, and scientific applications.

02

Data & Discrete

Statistics, probability, combinatorics, graph theory, algorithms, discrete structures, and mathematics for computing.

03

Proof & Structure

Introduction to Proofs and Abstract Algebra develop rigorous theorem construction, abstraction, algebraic structure, and research writing.

04

Analysis & Geometry

Real & Complex Analysis and Topology & Differential Geometry lead into rigorous advanced study of continuity, manifolds, curvature, and global structure.

A suggested progression—not a lockstep prerequisite chain.
