Administration Guide

Use 80% as the default mastery target. Require correction of every missed item and a written explanation of the theorem hypothesis, definition, or inference involved.

Academic integrity

Calculator, software, AI, and references must be disclosed. Learners must defend submitted proofs and cannot substitute generated text for understood reasoning.

Evidence

Retain exported assessment attempts, lesson records, investigations, portfolio artifacts, research notes, and oral-defense records.
