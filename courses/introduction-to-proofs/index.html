About Introduction to Proofs

Introduction to Proofs marks the transition from computational mathematics to rigorous mathematical argument. Students learn to read definitions carefully, construct proofs, critique reasoning, and communicate mathematical ideas with precision.

Course Features

Logic & LanguageStatements, quantifiers, implications, definitions, and notation establish a precise foundation for proof.
Proof MethodsDirect proof, contradiction, contrapositive, cases, existence, uniqueness, and counterexamples build a flexible proof toolkit.
Induction & StructureMathematical induction, recursion, sequences, and structural arguments connect proof with discrete and algebraic ideas.
Mathematical WritingStudents organize arguments, justify claims, revise proofs, and develop clear professional mathematical exposition.
