Contraposition, Contradiction & Minimal Counterexamples

Complete every item with definitions, visible reasoning, verified hypotheses, and a final limitation statement.

  1. 1. Develop proving an implication through the logically equivalent contrapositive. State definitions, hypotheses, strategy, theorem dependencies, conclusion, and verification.

  2. 2. Develop assuming the negation of a claim and deriving an impossibility. State definitions, hypotheses, strategy, theorem dependencies, conclusion, and verification.

  3. 3. Develop using reduced fractions, parity, and prime divisibility in classical arguments. State definitions, hypotheses, strategy, theorem dependencies, conclusion, and verification.

  4. 4. Develop showing that finite or nonexistent alternatives force contradictions. State definitions, hypotheses, strategy, theorem dependencies, conclusion, and verification.

  5. 5. Develop choosing a least failure and constructing a smaller one. State definitions, hypotheses, strategy, theorem dependencies, conclusion, and verification.

  6. 6. Develop selecting the indirect form that best exposes the mathematical mechanism. State definitions, hypotheses, strategy, theorem dependencies, conclusion, and verification.

  7. 7. Develop locating circularity, unjustified existence, and illicit cancellation. State definitions, hypotheses, strategy, theorem dependencies, conclusion, and verification.

  8. 8. Critique a flawed proof or theorem application related to Contraposition, Contradiction & Minimal Counterexamples.

  9. 9. Create a reproducible mini-investigation in number theory, impossibility results, optimization, uniqueness, and correctness proofs, documenting sources, examples, proof obligations, limitations, and future questions.
