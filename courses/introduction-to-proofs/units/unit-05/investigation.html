Indirect-Proof Strategy Comparison

When do indirect methods expose a proof more clearly than direct calculation?

Required evidence

  1. Precise question, definitions, domain, and notation
  2. Relevant prior theorems and a source map
  3. Examples, nonexamples, and boundary cases
  4. Proof strategy or analytic method with justified steps
  5. Counterexample search or hypothesis stress test
  6. Independent verification and documented revision
  7. Applications, limitations, reproducible appendix, and future questions

Application and research context

Number theory, impossibility results, optimization, uniqueness, and correctness proofs.

Document all sources, software, generated examples, diagrams, and AI assistance. Computation may support a conjecture or finite verification; it does not replace a general proof unless exhaustive coverage is itself justified.
