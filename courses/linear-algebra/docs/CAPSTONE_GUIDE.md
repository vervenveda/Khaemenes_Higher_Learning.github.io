# Linear Algebra Capstone Guide

The capstone must use a substantive matrix/vector model and include: a precise question; definitions and dimensions; data/source statement; method justification; exact or theoretical reasoning; computational implementation; verification; sensitivity or limitation analysis; a written memorandum; reproducible artifacts; and a 6–10 minute oral defense. Suitable domains include networks, transitions, graphics, data compression, least squares, ranking, coding, dynamical systems, or an original proof/computational study.
