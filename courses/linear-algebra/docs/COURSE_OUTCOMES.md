# Course Outcomes

- Systems, elimination, and matrix equations
- Matrix algebra, inverses, LU and QR structure
- Vector spaces, subspaces, basis, dimension, and rank-nullity
- Linear transformations, kernels, images, composition, and matrix representations
- Determinants and geometric scaling
- Eigenvalues, eigenvectors, diagonalization, and dynamics
- Inner products, projection, least squares, and orthogonalization
- Symmetric matrices, positive definiteness, quadratic forms, and spectral theorem
- SVD, pseudoinverse, low-rank approximation, PCA, and conditioning
- Applications, numerical methods, proof, reproducibility, and oral defense
