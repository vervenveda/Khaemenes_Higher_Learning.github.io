# Linear Algebra 36-Week Curriculum Map

## Unit 01 — Vectors, Linear Systems & Geometric Structure (Weeks 1–3)

**Essential question:** How do vectors, linear combinations, and systems of equations encode geometry, balance, and feasible solutions?

**Investigation:** Vector Geometry & Systems Modelling Portfolio

- Week 1: Vectors and Coordinates in Rⁿ; Dot Products, Norms, Distance & Angle; Linear Combinations and Span
- Week 2: Systems as Intersections and Constraints; The Matrix Equation Ax=b
- Week 3: Consistency and Parametric Solution Sets; Systems Modelling, Residuals & Verification
## Unit 02 — Matrix Algebra, Elimination & Invertibility (Weeks 4–6)

**Essential question:** How do row operations, matrix products, inverses, and elimination reveal solution structure and invertibility?

**Investigation:** Elimination, Inverse & LU Decision Laboratory

- Week 4: Elementary Row Operations and Echelon Form; Reduced Row Echelon Form and Pivot Structure; Matrix Addition, Multiplication & Composition
- Week 5: Inverse Matrices and Solving Ax=b; The Invertible Matrix Theorem
- Week 6: Elementary Matrices and Row-Operation Algebra; LU Factorization, Repeated Solves & Sensitivity
## Unit 03 — Matrix Factorizations & Computational Structure (Weeks 7–8)

**Essential question:** How do block structure, permutations, factorization, and computational cost turn matrix calculations into reliable algorithms?

**Investigation:** Matrix Factorization & Algorithm Design Studio

- Week 7: Block Matrices and Structured Products; Permutation Matrices and Pivoting; Computational Cost and Matrix Algorithms; LU with Partial Pivoting
- Week 8: QR Factorization Preview; Choosing a Factorization; Norms, Backward Error & Verification
## Unit 04 — Vector Spaces, Subspaces & Generated Structure (Weeks 9–11)

**Essential question:** How do vector-space axioms and subspaces unify coordinates, polynomials, functions, matrices, and solution sets?

**Investigation:** Four Fundamental Subspaces Concept Portfolio

- Week 9: Vector-Space Axioms and Examples; The Subspace Test; Column Space and Solvability
- Week 10: Nullspace and Homogeneous Solutions; Row Space, Left Nullspace & Orthogonality
- Week 11: Polynomial, Function & Matrix Spaces; Sums, Intersections & the Four Subspaces
## Unit 05 — Linear Independence, Basis, Dimension & Rank (Weeks 12–14)

**Essential question:** How do independence, basis, coordinates, dimension, and rank identify the essential degrees of freedom in a linear system?

**Investigation:** Basis, Coordinates & Rank–Nullity Proof Portfolio

- Week 12: Linear Independence and Dependence Relations; Spanning Sets and Basis Extraction; Coordinate Vectors Relative to a Basis
- Week 13: Dimension and Extension/Reduction Theorems; The Rank–Nullity Theorem
- Week 14: Change-of-Basis Matrices; Basis Construction Algorithms & Proof
## Unit 06 — Linear Transformations & Matrix Representations (Weeks 15–17)

**Essential question:** How do linear transformations preserve structure, and how do matrices encode them relative to chosen bases?

**Investigation:** Transformation, Kernel, Image & Representation Studio

- Week 15: Definition and Tests for Linearity; Kernel, Image & Rank–Nullity for Transformations; The Standard Matrix of a Transformation
- Week 16: Composition and Inverse Transformations; Geometric Transformations in the Plane and Space
- Week 17: Matrix Representations Relative to Bases; Isomorphisms, Similarity & Structural Equivalence
## Unit 07 — Determinants, Orientation & Volume (Weeks 18–19)

**Essential question:** How does the determinant measure invertibility, signed volume, orientation, and multiplicative scaling?

**Investigation:** Determinant Geometry & Proof Laboratory

- Week 18: Determinant Properties and Transformations; Cofactor Expansion and Recursive Structure; Determinants by Elimination; Signed Area, Volume & Orientation
- Week 19: Cramer’s Rule and Theoretical Solves; Determinant, Invertibility & Eigenvalue Zero; Multilinearity, Alternation & Proof
## Unit 08 — Eigenvalues, Eigenvectors & Diagonalization (Weeks 20–22)

**Essential question:** How do invariant directions and spectral structure reveal repeated action, dynamics, stability, and change of basis?

**Investigation:** Eigenstructure & Dynamical Systems Portfolio

- Week 20: Invariant Directions and Eigenpairs; Characteristic Polynomial and Spectrum; Eigenspaces and Multiplicity
- Week 21: Diagonalization Criteria and Construction; Matrix Powers, Difference Equations & Long-Term Behavior
- Week 22: Complex Eigenvalues, Rotations & Real Dynamics; Defective Matrices and Jordan-Form Preview
## Unit 09 — Inner Products, Orthogonality & Least Squares (Weeks 23–25)

**Essential question:** How do inner products, projections, orthogonal bases, and least squares produce closest approximations and stable coordinates?

**Investigation:** Orthogonality, QR & Data-Fitting Laboratory

- Week 23: Inner Products and Orthogonality; Orthogonal Complements and Decomposition; Projection onto a Vector or Subspace
- Week 24: Least Squares and the Normal Equations; Gram–Schmidt Orthonormalization
- Week 25: Orthogonal Matrices and QR Factorization; Linear Regression, Residual Diagnostics & Model Limits
## Unit 10 — Symmetric Matrices, Quadratic Forms & the Spectral Theorem (Weeks 26–28)

**Essential question:** How do symmetry, definiteness, quadratic forms, and orthogonal diagonalization reveal energy, curvature, and principal directions?

**Investigation:** Spectral Geometry & Quadratic-Form Analysis

- Week 26: Symmetric Matrices and Orthogonal Eigenvectors; The Spectral Theorem; Positive Definite Matrices and Tests
- Week 27: Quadratic Forms and Classification; Cholesky Factorization
- Week 28: Rayleigh Quotients and Constrained Optimization; Principal Axes, Covariance & Geometric Interpretation
## Unit 11 — Singular Value Decomposition & Data Geometry (Weeks 29–31)

**Essential question:** How does the singular value decomposition expose rank, geometry, conditioning, compression, and dominant data structure?

**Investigation:** SVD, Pseudoinverse & Low-Rank Data Laboratory

- Week 29: Constructing the Singular Value Decomposition; Geometry of the SVD; The Moore–Penrose Pseudoinverse
- Week 30: Low-Rank Approximation and Eckart–Young; Principal Component Analysis
- Week 31: Image Compression and Reconstruction; Conditioning, Noise & Truncated SVD
## Unit 12 — Applications: Markov Models, Networks, Graphics & Coding (Weeks 32–34)

**Essential question:** How does linear algebra organize transitions, networks, graphics, coding, ranking, and coupled dynamical systems?

**Investigation:** Applied Linear Algebra Systems Portfolio

- Week 32: Markov Chains and Stationary Distributions; Graphs, Incidence Matrices & Laplacians; Network Flows and Conservation
- Week 33: Computer Graphics and Homogeneous Coordinates; Error-Correcting Codes over Finite Fields
- Week 34: PageRank, Ranking & Recommendation Models; Coupled Differential Systems and Matrix Exponentials
## Unit 13 — Numerical Linear Algebra, Proof & Capstone (Weeks 35–36)

**Essential question:** How can rigorous proof, numerical evidence, sparse computation, and ethical modelling support a defensible linear-algebra capstone?

**Investigation:** College Linear Algebra Capstone & Oral Defense

- Week 35: Floating-Point Arithmetic and Error Propagation; Iterative Methods for Linear Systems; Sparse Matrices and Large-Scale Structure; Proof Strategies in Linear Algebra
- Week 36: Capstone Question, Data, Assumptions & Method; Capstone Computation, Verification & Reproducibility; Written Report and Oral Mathematical Defense; Second-Course Bridge: Abstract, Numerical & Applied Directions
