# Standards and Curriculum Union

The course is an original Khaemenes Higher Learning curriculum informed by the broad topic union of established first-course linear algebra programs. Its scope includes systems and matrix computation, abstract vector spaces, linear transformations, determinants, eigenstructure, orthogonality and least squares, symmetric and positive-definite matrices, SVD, numerical reasoning, and applications.

The course deliberately unites four modes of knowing:

1. **Computational:** algorithms, row operations, factorizations, and numerical checks.
2. **Conceptual:** span, independence, subspace, basis, dimension, kernel, image, and spectral structure.
3. **Geometric:** vectors, projections, volume, invariant directions, ellipsoids, and transformations.
4. **Applied and communicative:** modelling, data, networks, graphics, error analysis, proof, and oral defense.

No official affiliation, accreditation, or institutional endorsement is claimed.
