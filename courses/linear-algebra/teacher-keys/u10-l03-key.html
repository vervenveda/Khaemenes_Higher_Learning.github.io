Positive Definite Matrices and Tests

Reward correct mathematics, explicit dimensions/hypotheses, interpretation, and verification. Equivalent valid reasoning should receive credit.

Problem 1

Yes; xᵀAx=2x₁²+5x₂²>0 for nonzero x.

Problem 2

For a symmetric matrix, all positive leading principal minors are equivalent to positive definiteness.

Problem 3

Definitions should identify the vector/matrix/space/transformation, dimensions or domain/codomain, and the hypotheses behind recognizing strictly positive quadratic energy. Use the formulas xᵀAx>0 for x≠0; symmetric A positive definite iff all eigenvalues positive.

Problem 4

A strong error analysis identifies the first step that violates a definition, dimension, theorem hypothesis, or matrix-operation rule; then repairs that step and re-verifies the final result.

Problem 5

Application responses vary. Require defined variables, a compatible matrix/vector model connected to energy models, covariance, optimization, conic classification, stability, and mechanics, a complete computation, interpretation, and limitations.

Problem 6

Accept two independent checks appropriate to the task: reconstruction, residual, RREF comparison, determinant/rank test, orthogonality, substitution, eigenpair check Av=λv, factorization product, or a second method.

Scoring guidance

Six problems × 10 points: 5 mathematical accuracy, 2 structure/justification, 2 verification/interpretation, 1 notation and communication.
