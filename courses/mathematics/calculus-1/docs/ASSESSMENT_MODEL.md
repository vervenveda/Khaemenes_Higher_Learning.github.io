# Assessment Model

Suggested weighting:
- Weekly classwork and corrected practice: 35%
- Unit mastery evidence: 20%
- Midterm examination: 15%
- Comprehensive final examination: 20%
- Capstone and oral defense: 10%

The course includes a 40-question readiness diagnostic, 13 randomized mastery checks, a 60-question midterm, a 100-question final, 13 investigations, 92 printable lessons, 92 keys, and portfolio evidence. The mastery target is 80%. Reassessment follows reteaching, correction, and reflection.
