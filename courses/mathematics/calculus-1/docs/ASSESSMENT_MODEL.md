# Calculus I · Assessment and Progression Contract

**Academy policy: 80% mastery is mandatory for progression.** This is not a suggested target and is not replaced by a weighted course average.

## Sequential progression (patterned after Khaemenes Pre-Algebra)

- The current lesson remains open for instruction, correction, review and free practice.
- The next lesson or activity does not unlock until the preceding lesson's assessed mastery reaches **at least 80%**.
- An entire new unit remains gated until **every required lesson** within the prior unit has met 80% **and** the prior unit mastery check reaches at least 80%.
- Required weekly quizzes, projects, labs, midterm, final and capstone each maintain their own 80% progression/completion threshold where designated; a strong score elsewhere cannot compensate for a missing or failed essential gate.
- Retakes, instruction, reasonable accommodations, explanations and remediation remain free to all learners.
- The best demonstrated mastery is retained for progression, with the actual attempt form and selected answer order saved separately by the local Calculus I learner identity.

## Implemented versus pending academic review

**Implemented in the October 18, 2026 Calculus I hardening pass:**

1. Unit 01's **seven lessons** have 10-question, varied, lesson-specific formative mastery checks.
2. Next-lesson routes require a saved score of **80% or higher**. Locked direct routes display a clear prerequisite link.
3. The Unit 01 cumulative check is a 21-question form distributed across seven lesson topics. It remains locked until all seven lesson checkpoints pass.
4. Unit 02's entry requires **all seven** Unit 01 lesson scores and the Unit 01 cumulative check at **80% or higher**.
5. Course dashboard and all 13 unit home pages apply navigation/previous-unit checks. Later lesson URLs require previous mastery; unreviewed pages retain a free **study-preview mode** without counting progress.
6. Progress is stored locally under the selected Calculus I course learner. Quiz snapshots include question identity, answer option order, selected option, score, time and pass status. Subject/faculty review of question-bank accuracy is still required.

**Not yet complete:** Lesson-specific reviewed assessments for Units 02–13, full activity-level gates for every lab and exercise, independent mathematics peer review, secure unseen high-stakes examinations, approved profile/registrar binding, formal credit/certificate verification, and live browser/mobile accessibility acceptance. The next lesson in unreleased units stays pending rather than silently receiving a fabricated mastery score.

## Previous weighting (informational only)

A gradebook may show the preexisting informational weighted average:

- Weekly classwork and corrected practice: 35%
- Unit mastery evidence: 20%
- Midterm examination: 15%
- Comprehensive final examination: 20%
- Capstone and oral defense: 10%

**This percentage cannot open a locked lesson, unit, course completion record, or credential.** All designated gates must independently pass 80%, regardless of the weighted average.

## Assessment integrity and academic authority

Public JavaScript quizzes and locally saved scores provide **formative progression**, not secure summative or accreditor-recognized proof. The existing 156-question bank has repeated generic distractors and requires mathematics review across the remaining units. Unit 01's new generators are an improved *candidate practice bank*, not independent faculty-approved exam items.

Do not treat entering a score manually, checking off a lesson, altering localStorage, importing a student backup, or printing a completion template as valid faculty-verified academic mastery. Real institutional certificate/credit decisions require properly authorized administration and independent assessment review.
