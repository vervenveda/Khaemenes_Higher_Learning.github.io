# Calculator and CAD Policy

Calculator and CAD use is permitted when the task measures modelling, interpretation, graph analysis, numerical approximation, design, or verification. It may be restricted when the target is exact algebraic, limit, derivative, or antiderivative reasoning.

Every technology-supported submission must show:
1. the mathematical setup,
2. relevant window, scale, unit, or tolerance settings,
3. the resulting evidence,
4. a written interpretation,
5. an independent reasonableness check.
