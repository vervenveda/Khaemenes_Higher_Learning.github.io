# Khaemenes Academy Calculus I — Curriculum Map

**36 weeks · 180 study sessions · 13 units · 92 detailed lessons**

Open-age, placement-based course. Prerequisite: Precalculus and Trigonometry readiness or equivalent demonstrated mastery.

## Course purpose

Develop conceptual, procedural, graphical, numerical, contextual, and modelling fluency in first-course differential and integral calculus. The course is AP Calculus AB-informed and first-semester-college-calculus aligned, but the repository does not claim College Board AP authorization.

## Unit sequence

### Unit 01 — Calculus Readiness & Function Fluency
- Weeks: 1, 2, 3
- Essential question: How do function structure, representations, units, and algebraic fluency prepare us to analyze change and accumulation?
- Investigation: Readiness Portfolio & Function Behavior Defense
- Lessons:
  1. Function Language, Units & Multiple Representations (Week 1)
  2. Domains, Ranges, Intercepts & End Behavior (Week 1)
  3. Transformations, Composition & Inverse Structure (Week 1)
  4. Average Rate of Change & Difference Quotients (Week 2)
  5. Algebraic Techniques for Calculus (Week 2)
  6. Trigonometric, Exponential & Logarithmic Fluency (Week 3)
  7. Modelling Assumptions, Technology & Verification (Week 3)

### Unit 02 — Limits: Numerical, Graphical & Algebraic Reasoning
- Weeks: 4, 5, 6

- Lessons:
  1. Approaching a Value: Tables, Graphs & Language (Week 4)
  2. One-Sided Limits and Two-Sided Agreement (Week 4)
  3. Limits from Graphs with Holes and Jumps (Week 4)

  5. Indeterminate Forms and Algebraic Simplification (Week 5)
  6. Rationalization and Radical Limits (Week 5)
  7. Infinite Limits and Vertical Asymptotes (Week 6)
  8. Limits at Infinity and End Behavior (Week 6)

### Unit 03 — Continuity & Asymptotic Behavior
- Weeks: 7, 8, 9

  2. Classifying Discontinuities (Week 7)

  4. The Intermediate Value Theorem (Week 8)
  5. The Squeeze Theorem (Week 8)
  6. Continuity of Composite and Transcendental Functions (Week 9)
  7. Continuity, Asymptotes & Model Reliability (Week 9)

### Unit 04 — The Derivative from First Principles
