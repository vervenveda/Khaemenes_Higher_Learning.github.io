# Research and Course-Design Notes

Calculus I is positioned after Precalculus & Trigonometry and before a future Calculus II course. The sequence preserves the first-course core: limits, continuity, derivative definition and rules, derivative applications, Riemann sums, the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, antiderivatives, substitution, differential equations, and integral applications.

Optional Calculus II bridges—such as integration by parts—are clearly labelled and do not displace the Calculus I core.

The phrase “AP-informed” describes curricular overlap only. Official AP course naming requires a separate College Board Course Audit and authorization process.
