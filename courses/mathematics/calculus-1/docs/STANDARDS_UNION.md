# Calculus I Standards Union

This course is designed as a rigorous first course in differential and integral calculus for prepared high-school, homeschool, adult, and independent learners.

## Content domains

1. Function fluency and mathematical modelling
2. Limits and continuity
3. Definition and interpretation of the derivative
4. Differentiation rules, implicit differentiation, and inverse functions
5. Contextual and analytical applications of derivatives
6. Riemann sums, definite integrals, and accumulation
7. Fundamental Theorem of Calculus and antiderivatives
8. Substitution and introductory integration strategy
9. Differential equations, slope fields, separable models, and Euler's method
10. Area, volume, average value, optimization, motion, and applied modelling
11. Mathematical communication, justification, validation, and strategic technology use

## Design references

The scope reflects the durable overlap among:
- College Board AP Calculus AB content and mathematical practices
- First-semester college Calculus I expectations
- State advanced-mathematics and calculus frameworks where offered
- Cambridge International AS & A Level Mathematics calculus expectations
- International Baccalaureate Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches calculus expectations
- Common mathematical-practice expectations: reasoning, argument, modelling, tools, structure, and precision

This is an alignment document, not a claim of automatic AP, IB, Cambridge, state, district, or college authorization. Programs should map the course to their current local requirements before awarding a particular designation.
