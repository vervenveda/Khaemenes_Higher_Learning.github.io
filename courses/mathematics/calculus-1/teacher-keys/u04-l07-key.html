Unit 04 · Lesson 07 · Week 12 · The Derivative from First Principles

Estimating and Sketching Derivatives from Graphs

A derivative graph records the slope of the original function. Positive derivative values correspond to increasing behavior, zeros often correspond to horizontal tangents, and derivative magnitude reflects steepness. This lesson emphasizes estimating and sketching derivatives from graphs through symbolic, graphical, numerical, and contextual evidence.

Open-AgePrintableFoundation · Core · ExtendedMastery target 80%

Learning intentions

  • Define and use the central calculus language in estimating and sketching derivatives from graphs.
  • Move among symbolic, graphical, numerical, and contextual representations.
  • Show complete reasoning with correct notation, domain conditions, units, and intervals.
  • Verify a conclusion using a second method or representation.

Essential question

How does the limiting slope of secant lines define instantaneous change?

Visual model

A derivative graph records the slope of the original function. Positive derivative values correspond to increasing behavior, zeros often correspond to horizontal tangents, and derivative magnitude reflects steepness.

f'(x)>0f'(x)<0f'(x)=0

Worked reasoning

Sign mapScan the original graph from left to right and label intervals of positive, zero, and negative slope.
Shape comparisonUse turning points and steepness to place key features on the derivative graph.

Misconception check: The derivative graph does not copy the height of the original graph; it records its slope.

Using first principles, find f'(4) for f(x)=x².

Answer: 8
Reasoning: The derivative of x² from the difference quotient is 2x; at 4 it is 8.
2

Write the tangent-line equation to y=x² at x=1.

Answer: y−1=2(x−1)
Reasoning: The point is (1,1) and the derivative is 2x.
3

What does f'(a)>0 indicate locally?

Answer: f is increasing near a.
Reasoning: A positive derivative means positive tangent slope.
4

Name a point where |x| is not differentiable.

Answer: x=0
Reasoning: The graph has a corner at the origin.
5

If position is measured in meters and time in seconds, give velocity units.

Answer: meters per second
Reasoning: Derivative units are output units divided by input units.
6

What feature of f often corresponds to a zero of f'?

Answer: A horizontal tangent or stationary point
Reasoning: Zero derivative means tangent slope zero.
