Essential question

How can derivatives, integrals, and differential equations support a defensible analysis of a complex problem?

Visual model

Integral applications begin with geometry and slicing. The variable of integration determines slice orientation; every area or volume element must be expressed consistently in that variable.

A=\int_a^b(\text{top}-\text{bottom})dxV=\int_a^b A(x)dxV=\pi\int_a^b(R^2-r^2)dx

Worked reasoning

Sketch and intersectionsGraph boundaries, solve intersection points, and identify the positive geometric quantity being accumulated.
Slice expressionWrite one representative slice before integrating and include squared radii where required.

Misconception check: For area between curves, the integrand must remain nonnegative on the selected interval or the interval must be split.

Find the area between y=x and y=x² on [0,1].

Answer: 1/6

Write a washer integral with outer radius R and inner radius r.

Answer: π∫(R²−r²)dx
Reasoning: Washer area is π(R²−r²).
3

A solid has square cross sections of side x on [0,2]. Find its volume.

Answer: 8/3
Reasoning: Integrate x² from 0 to 2.
4

Find the average value of f(x)=x on [0,4].

Answer: 2
Reasoning: (1/4)∫₀⁴x dx=2.
5

Why sketch before integrating?

Answer: It identifies bounds, orientation, and positive dimensions.
Reasoning: The geometry determines the integrand.
6

When must area-between-curves intervals be split?

Answer: When the top and bottom curves switch.
Reasoning: The geometric height must remain nonnegative.
