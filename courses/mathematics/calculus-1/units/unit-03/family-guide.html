Continuity & Asymptotic Behavior

How do continuity conditions connect local behavior, global guarantees, and model reliability?

Before the unit

Review prerequisite algebra and trigonometry, confirm calculator access, and complete any Foundation tasks indicated by the readiness evidence.

During the unit

Ask the learner to explain notation aloud, retain corrected worksheets, label units, and verify technology output with independent reasoning.

Evidence to retain

  • One corrected lesson worksheet
  • One mastery attempt with corrections
  • The applied investigation and rubric
  • A reflection describing next steps

Mastery expectation

Target 80% with explanation. A lower first score is diagnostic; reteach, correct, and reassess rather than averaging uncorrected attempts.

Unit vocabulary families

asymptotes, continuity, limits
