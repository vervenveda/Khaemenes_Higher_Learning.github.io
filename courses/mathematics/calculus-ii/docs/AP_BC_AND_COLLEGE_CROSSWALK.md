# AP Calculus BC-Informed and College Calculus II Crosswalk

This is an informational crosswalk, not an AP authorization claim.

| Khaemenes unit | AP Calculus BC-informed connection | Broader college Calculus II contribution |
|---|---|---|
| 01 Calculus I Bridge | Fundamental Theorem, accumulation, substitution, justification | prerequisite diagnosis, units, strategy triage, verification |
| 02 Integration by Parts | advanced antidifferentiation | repeated/tabular methods, reduction, cyclic integrals, proof checks |
| 03 Trigonometric Methods | integration techniques | systematic trigonometric identities, all three radical substitution families, domain analysis |
| 04 Partial Fractions | rational-function integration | polynomial division, repeated and irreducible factors, coefficient systems, domain-safe CAS comparison |
| 05 Improper and Numerical Integration | numerical approximation and integration reasoning | infinite intervals, unbounded integrands, comparison, midpoint, trapezoid, Simpson, refinement and error |
| 06 Advanced Applications | areas, volumes, accumulation | general slicing, washers, shells, arc length, surface area, dimensional verification |
| 07 Physical and Probability Applications | real-world integral modelling | work, pumping, hydrostatic force, mass, centroids, density functions, expectation and variance |
| 08 Differential Equations | slope fields, Euler method, separable models | first-order linear equations, integrating factors, equilibria, residuals and parameter estimation |
| 09 Parametric and Vector-Valued Motion | parametric and vector-valued functions | orientation, second derivatives, area, length, velocity, acceleration, trajectory modelling |
| 10 Polar Calculus | polar coordinates and calculus | intersections, pole behavior, slope, loops, area, arc length, conics and spirals |
| 11 Sequence and Series Foundations | infinite sequences and series | partial-sum logic, telescoping, p-series, integral-test remainders and approximation |
| 12 Convergence Tests | convergence and error | direct/limit comparison, alternating error, absolute/conditional behavior, ratio/root selection logic |
| 13 Power and Taylor Series | power series and polynomial approximation | endpoint testing, operations, rigorous remainder bounds, integration/ODE applications, capstone synthesis |

## Practice crosswalk

- implementing mathematical processes → method classification and execution;
- connecting representations → symbolic, graphical, numerical, verbal, and contextual evidence;
- justification → hypotheses, domains, convergence conditions, error bounds, counterexamples, and verification;
- communication and notation → complete derivations, units, interpretation, limitations, and oral defense;
- technology use → calculator, interactive labs, graphing, numerical approximation, and CAD with a preserved reasoning trail.
