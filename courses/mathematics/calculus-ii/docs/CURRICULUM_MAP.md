# Calculus II Curriculum Map

**Course:** Khaemenes Higher Learning Calculus II  
**Length:** 36 weeks · 180 sessions · 92 lessons  
**Prerequisite:** Calculus I or equivalent demonstrated mastery  
**Pathways:** Foundation · Core · Honors / Extended  

## Sequence

### Unit 01 · Calculus I Bridge & Integration Fluency — Weeks 1, 2
How can the Fundamental Theorem, substitution, algebraic structure, units, and verification restore fluent integration reasoning?

- 01. Antiderivative Families, Constants & Initial Conditions
- 02. The Fundamental Theorem as an Evaluation and Accumulation Principle
- 03. Net Change, Total Change & Units
- 04. Substitution with Algebraic Structure
- 05. Substitution with Exponential, Logarithmic & Trigonometric Forms
- 06. Integration Strategy Triage
- 07. Readiness Synthesis, Error Analysis & Technology Verification

**Investigation:** Integration Readiness Portfolio & Oral Method Defense

### Unit 02 · Integration by Parts, Reduction & Cyclic Structure — Weeks 3, 4, 5
How does reversing the product rule transform products, recursive integrals, and cyclic structures into solvable forms?

- 01. Reversing the Product Rule
- 02. Choosing u and dv Strategically
- 03. Repeated and Tabular Integration by Parts
- 04. Definite Integration by Parts
- 05. Reduction Formulas & Recursive Integrals
- 06. Cyclic Exponential–Trigonometric Integrals
- 07. Applications, Method Comparison & Proof of Verification

**Investigation:** Product-Rule Reversal Laboratory

### Unit 03 · Trigonometric Integrals & Trigonometric Substitution — Weeks 6, 7, 8
How do identities and right-triangle substitutions convert trigonometric powers and radical forms into integrable structure?

- 01. Powers of Sine and Cosine
- 02. Powers of Tangent and Secant
- 03. Products, Multiple Angles & Identity Selection
- 04. Substitution for √(a²−x²)
- 05. Substitution for √(a²+x²)
- 06. Substitution for √(x²−a²)
- 07. Back-Substitution, Domain Analysis & Verification

**Investigation:** Identity-to-Substitution Decision Atlas

### Unit 04 · Rational Integration & Partial Fractions — Weeks 9, 10, 11
How does algebraic decomposition expose the logarithmic, inverse-trigonometric, and rational pieces hidden inside a rational function?

- 01. Polynomial Division Before Decomposition
- 02. Distinct Linear Factors
- 03. Repeated Linear Factors
- 04. Irreducible Quadratic Factors
- 05. Repeated Irreducible Quadratics
- 06. Mixed Factorizations & Coefficient Systems
- 07. Strategy, CAS Comparison & Domain-Safe Verification

**Investigation:** Rational Decomposition & Verification Studio

### Unit 05 · Improper Integrals & Numerical Integration — Weeks 12, 13, 14
How do limits, comparison, numerical methods, and error bounds extend integration beyond elementary antiderivatives and finite intervals?

- 01. Infinite-Interval Improper Integrals
- 02. Unbounded Integrands on Finite Intervals
- 03. Comparison for Improper Integrals
- 04. p-Integrals & Threshold Reasoning
- 05. Midpoint & Trapezoidal Rules
- 06. Simpson’s Rule & Error Structure
- 07. Adaptive Strategy, Nonelementary Integrals & Validation

**Investigation:** Convergence & Numerical Error Laboratory

### Unit 06 · Advanced Applications of Integration — Weeks 15, 16, 17
How do geometry, slicing, rotation, and metric formulas turn integral setup into defensible area, volume, length, and surface models?

- 01. Area Between Curves & Intersection Analysis
- 02. Volumes by General Slicing
- 03. Disks & Washers
- 04. Cylindrical Shells
- 05. Arc Length
- 06. Surface Area of Revolution
- 07. Method Selection, Units & Geometric Verification

**Investigation:** Integrated Design: Area, Volume & Surface Optimization

### Unit 07 · Physical, Engineering & Probability Applications — Weeks 18, 19, 20
How does integration combine distributed force, mass, pressure, probability, and expectation into measurable system quantities?

- 01. Work by a Variable Force & Hooke’s Law
- 02. Pumping Fluids & Distributed Lifting
- 03. Hydrostatic Force on Submerged Surfaces
- 04. Moments, Centroids & Centers of Mass
- 05. Probability Density Functions
- 06. Expected Value, Variance & Risk Interpretation
- 07. Engineering Model Assumptions, Sensitivity & Reporting

**Investigation:** Applied Integration Engineering Memorandum

### Unit 08 · Differential Equations & Numerical Models — Weeks 21, 22, 23
How do direction fields, exact methods, numerical methods, and parameter evidence describe systems that change over time?

- 01. Direction Fields, Equilibria & Qualitative Behavior
- 02. Euler’s Method & Global Error
- 03. Separable Differential Equations
- 04. Exponential Growth, Decay & Half-Life
- 05. Logistic Growth & Carrying Capacity
- 06. First-Order Linear Equations & Integrating Factors
- 07. Parameter Estimation, Residuals & Model Comparison

**Investigation:** Dynamic-System Model & Parameter Defense

### Unit 09 · Parametric Curves & Vector-Valued Motion — Weeks 24, 25, 26
How do parameters and vectors represent orientation, motion, tangent behavior, curvature evidence, area, and length beyond single-valued graphs?

- 01. Parametric Representation, Elimination & Orientation
- 02. First Derivatives & Tangent Lines
- 03. Second Derivatives & Concavity
- 04. Area from Parametric Curves
- 05. Parametric Arc Length
- 06. Vector-Valued Position, Velocity & Acceleration
- 07. Projectile, Cycloid & Trajectory Modelling

**Investigation:** Parametric Motion & Trajectory Laboratory

### Unit 10 · Polar Coordinates & Polar Calculus — Weeks 27, 28, 29
How do radial coordinates reveal symmetry, periodicity, intersections, tangents, areas, lengths, and conic structure?

- 01. Polar Coordinates, Equivalence & Conversion
- 02. Graphing Polar Curves & Symmetry Tests
- 03. Polar Derivatives & Tangent Slopes
- 04. Area in Polar Coordinates
- 05. Intersections, Loops & Overlap
- 06. Polar Arc Length
- 07. Polar Conics, Spirals & Applied Modelling

**Investigation:** Polar Design & Measurement Portfolio

### Unit 11 · Sequences & Infinite Series Foundations — Weeks 30, 31, 32
How do sequence behavior, partial sums, comparison models, and remainder estimates determine whether infinite accumulation has a finite value?

- 01. Sequence Limits & Long-Run Behavior
- 02. Monotone, Bounded & Recursive Sequences
- 03. Infinite Series & Partial-Sum Sequences
- 04. Geometric & Telescoping Series
- 05. The nth-Term Divergence Test
- 06. Integral Test, p-Series & Continuous Comparison
- 07. Integral-Test Remainders & Approximation

**Investigation:** Infinite Process Evidence Notebook

### Unit 12 · Convergence Tests & Conditional Behavior — Weeks 33, 34
How can comparison, alternation, ratios, roots, and absolute convergence classify series and quantify approximation reliability?

- 01. Direct Comparison Test
- 02. Limit Comparison Test
- 03. Alternating Series & Error Bounds
- 04. Absolute vs Conditional Convergence
- 05. Ratio Test
- 06. Root Test
- 07. Test Selection, Counterexamples & Decision Logic

**Investigation:** Convergence Decision Tree & Counterexample Defense

### Unit 13 · Power Series, Taylor Series & Modelling Capstone — Weeks 35, 36
How do power series represent functions, control approximation error, solve problems, and bridge single-variable calculus to advanced analysis?

- 01. Power Series & Radius of Convergence
- 02. Endpoint Testing & Interval of Convergence
- 03. Operations on Power Series
- 04. Taylor Polynomials & Local Approximation
- 05. Benchmark Maclaurin Series
- 06. Taylor Remainder & Error Bounds
- 07. Series in Integration & Differential Equations
- 08. Capstone Synthesis, Oral Defense & Calculus III Bridge

**Investigation:** College Calculus II Modelling Capstone & Oral Defense
