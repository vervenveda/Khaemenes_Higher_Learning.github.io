# Standards Union and Collegiate Alignment

This original course is designed as a rigorous second-semester college calculus sequence. It aligns its scope and mathematical practices with:

- the current AP Calculus BC framework, especially advanced integration, differential equations, applications of integration, parametric equations, polar coordinates, vector-valued functions, and infinite sequences and series;
- the nationwide general-calculus scope represented by OpenStax Calculus Volume 2: integration, applications, techniques, differential equations, sequences and series, power series, and parametric/polar equations;
- common university Calculus II outcomes: method selection, exact and numerical integration, convergence analysis, approximation error, modelling, interpretation, and communication.

The course is **AP Calculus BC-informed**, not AP-authorized. It uses original lessons and questions and does not reproduce College Board exam items.

## Mathematical practice expectations

1. Represent problems graphically, numerically, analytically, verbally, and contextually.
2. State definitions, hypotheses, domains, units, and limitations.
3. Build and justify arguments rather than report unsupported answers.
4. Select technology intentionally and preserve a non-technology reasoning trail.
5. Verify results by an independent method.
6. Analyze error, convergence, sensitivity, and reasonableness.
7. Communicate complete mathematics in a professional portfolio.
