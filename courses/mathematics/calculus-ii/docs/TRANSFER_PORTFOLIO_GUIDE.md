# Transfer and Prior-Learning Portfolio Guide

This repository does not award accredited college credit. A learner seeking placement, prior-learning review, dual-enrollment recognition, or transfer consideration may assemble an evidence portfolio for the receiving institution.

Recommended contents:

1. course syllabus and curriculum map;
2. dated pacing and attendance or study-session record;
3. diagnostic, midterm, final, and corrected work;
4. all thirteen investigation summaries;
5. representative lesson worksheets and faculty-scored rubrics;
6. capstone report, data, code or tool record, and oral-defense rubric;
7. gradebook, standards-mastery record, and signed completion record;
8. a statement identifying the evaluator and assessment conditions.

The receiving institution alone determines whether any evidence qualifies for placement or credit.
