Faculty Key · Reduction Formulas & Recursive Integrals

Building recurrences that lower powers or complexity. This collegiate lesson requires exact reasoning, conditions, interpretation, and independent verification.

Foundation · Core · Honors / ExtendedOriginal problemsPrintableFaculty key

Learning intentions

  • Explain building recurrences that lower powers or complexity.
  • Carry out a complete symbolic or numerical solution with conditions and units.
  • Compare at least two representations or methods.
  • Diagnose a plausible error and verify the repaired result.
I_n relates to I_{n−1} or I_{n−2}

Concept development

Reduction Formulas & Recursive Integrals belongs to the broader Calculus II practice of selecting structure before computation. The method is not complete until its hypotheses, domain, units, and interpretation have been addressed.

1 · Classify

Identify the mathematical structure and target quantity.

2 · Select

Choose a method whose hypotheses are satisfied.

3 · Execute

Show transformations with exact notation and units.

4 · Validate

Use an independent check and interpret the result.

Worked reasoning A

Problem: Derive I_n=∫x^n e^x dx in terms of I_{n−1}.

Solution: I_n=x^n e^x−nI_{n−1}.

Worked reasoning B

Problem: Use reduction to evaluate ∫x³e^x dx.

Solution: e^x(x³−3x²+6x−6)+C.
