Concept development

Hydrostatic Force on Submerged Surfaces belongs to the broader Calculus II practice of selecting structure before computation. The method is not complete until its hypotheses, domain, units, and interpretation have been addressed.

1 · Classify

Identify the mathematical structure and target quantity.

2 · Select

Choose a method whose hypotheses are satisfied.

3 · Execute

Show transformations with exact notation and units.

4 · Validate

Use an independent check and interpret the result.

Worked reasoning A

Problem: A vertical 2 m by 3 m plate has its top at water surface. Find force.

Solution: F=9800·2∫₀³y dy=88200 N.

Worked reasoning B

Problem: Why does pressure increase linearly with depth?

Solution: The weight of fluid above a unit area is proportional to depth.
