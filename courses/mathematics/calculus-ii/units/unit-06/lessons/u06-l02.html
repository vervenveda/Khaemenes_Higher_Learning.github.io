Volumes by General Slicing

Expressing volume as the integral of cross-sectional area. This collegiate lesson requires exact reasoning, conditions, interpretation, and independent verification.

Foundation · Core · Honors / ExtendedOriginal problemsPrintable

Learning intentions

  • Explain expressing volume as the integral of cross-sectional area.
  • Carry out a complete symbolic or numerical solution with conditions and units.
  • Compare at least two representations or methods.
  • Diagnose a plausible error and verify the repaired result.
V=∫A(x)dx

Concept development

Volumes by General Slicing belongs to the broader Calculus II practice of selecting structure before computation. The method is not complete until its hypotheses, domain, units, and interpretation have been addressed.

1 · Classify

Identify the mathematical structure and target quantity.

2 · Select

Choose a method whose hypotheses are satisfied.

3 · Execute

Show transformations with exact notation and units.

4 · Validate

Use an independent check and interpret the result.

Worked reasoning A

Problem: A solid has square cross-sections perpendicular to x-axis over 0≤x≤2 with side x. Find V.

Solution: ∫₀²x²dx=8/3.

Worked reasoning B

Problem: If cross-sections are semicircles with diameter f(x), what is A(x)?

Solution: A(x)=πf(x)²/8.
