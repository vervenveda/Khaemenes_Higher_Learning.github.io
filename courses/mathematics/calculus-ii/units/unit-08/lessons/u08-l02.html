Euler’s Method & Global Error

Building numerical solution estimates from local slopes. This collegiate lesson requires exact reasoning, conditions, interpretation, and independent verification.

Foundation · Core · Honors / ExtendedOriginal problemsPrintable

Learning intentions

  • Explain building numerical solution estimates from local slopes.
  • Carry out a complete symbolic or numerical solution with conditions and units.
  • Compare at least two representations or methods.
  • Diagnose a plausible error and verify the repaired result.

Concept development

Euler’s Method & Global Error belongs to the broader Calculus II practice of selecting structure before computation. The method is not complete until its hypotheses, domain, units, and interpretation have been addressed.

1 · Classify

Identify the mathematical structure and target quantity.

2 · Select

Choose a method whose hypotheses are satisfied.

3 · Execute

Show transformations with exact notation and units.

4 · Validate

Use an independent check and interpret the result.

Worked reasoning A

Problem: Use one Euler step with h=0.1 for y′=x+y, y(0)=1.

Solution: y₁=1+0.1(1)=1.1 at x=.1.

Worked reasoning B

Problem: Use a second step.

Solution: Slope=.1+1.1=1.2, so y₂=1.22.
