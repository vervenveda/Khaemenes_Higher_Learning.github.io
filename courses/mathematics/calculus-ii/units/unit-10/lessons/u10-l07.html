Polar Conics, Spirals & Applied Modelling

Interpreting focus-directrix forms and radial growth. This collegiate lesson requires exact reasoning, conditions, interpretation, and independent verification.

Learning intentions

  • Explain interpreting focus-directrix forms and radial growth.
  • Carry out a complete symbolic or numerical solution with conditions and units.
  • Compare at least two representations or methods.
  • Diagnose a plausible error and verify the repaired result.

Concept development

Polar Conics, Spirals & Applied Modelling belongs to the broader Calculus II practice of selecting structure before computation. The method is not complete until its hypotheses, domain, units, and interpretation have been addressed.

1 · Classify

Identify the mathematical structure and target quantity.

2 · Select

Choose a method whose hypotheses are satisfied.

3 · Execute

Show transformations with exact notation and units.

4 · Validate

Use an independent check and interpret the result.

Worked reasoning A

Problem: Classify a conic with eccentricity e=1/2.

Solution: Ellipse.

Worked reasoning B

Problem: What does r=a+bθ describe?

Solution: An Archimedean spiral with constant radial increase per unit angle.
