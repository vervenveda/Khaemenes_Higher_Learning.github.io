Alternating Series & Error Bounds

Using decreasing magnitudes and a zero limit. This collegiate lesson requires exact reasoning, conditions, interpretation, and independent verification.

Learning intentions

  • Explain using decreasing magnitudes and a zero limit.
  • Carry out a complete symbolic or numerical solution with conditions and units.
  • Compare at least two representations or methods.
  • Diagnose a plausible error and verify the repaired result.

Concept development

Alternating Series & Error Bounds belongs to the broader Calculus II practice of selecting structure before computation. The method is not complete until its hypotheses, domain, units, and interpretation have been addressed.

1 · Classify

Identify the mathematical structure and target quantity.

2 · Select

Choose a method whose hypotheses are satisfied.

3 · Execute

Show transformations with exact notation and units.

4 · Validate

Use an independent check and interpret the result.

Worked reasoning A

Problem: Test Σ(−1)^{n+1}/n.

Solution: Converges by the alternating-series test.

Worked reasoning B

Problem: How many terms ensure error below .01?

Solution: Need 1/(N+1)<.01, so N≥100.
