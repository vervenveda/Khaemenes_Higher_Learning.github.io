# Calculator, CAD & Technology Policy

Technology is used to explore, verify, model, and communicate—not to replace symbolic reasoning.

Students should record the equation or model, substitutions, window or domain, units, result, interpretation, and one verification step. Calculator-active work includes regression, numerical equation solving, trigonometric graphs, polar and parametric curves, matrices, sequences, and limits.

OHMIC CAD may support vector components, bearings, rotation, scale, coordinate design, and capstone diagrams. CAD exports must be accompanied by mathematical analysis.
