# Precalculus & Trigonometry Standards Union

This course synthesizes advanced high-school and introductory college-precalculus expectations without claiming automatic AP authorization or college credit.

## Primary framework

- College Board AP Precalculus: polynomial and rational functions; exponential and logarithmic functions; trigonometric and polar functions; functions involving parameters, vectors, and matrices.
- AP mathematical practices: procedural and symbolic fluency, multiple representations, and communication and reasoning.
- AP curricular emphasis on model selection, construction, validation, graphing-calculator access, and defensible conclusions.

## Additional alignment lenses

- Florida B.E.S.T. Mathematics mathematical thinking and reasoning practices and advanced course expectations.
- Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for precalculus and mathematical process standards.
- Common Core high-school functions, number and quantity, algebra, geometry, and statistics/modeling progressions.
- International Baccalaureate mathematics analysis/approaches and applications/interpretation emphases on inquiry, technology, modelling, and communication.
- Calculus-readiness concepts: covariation, average rate of change, difference quotients, limits, continuity, and multiple representations.

## Course distinction

Units 1–9 provide the central advanced-functions and trigonometry spine. Units 10–13 extend beyond a minimal AP Precalculus sequence through series, conics, limits, and a documented modelling capstone. Schools seeking the AP designation must independently complete the College Board Course Audit.
