About Precalculus & Trigonometry

This course connects advanced algebraic functions with trigonometric reasoning and the representations students need before calculus. The emphasis is on structure, modelling, multiple representations, and mathematical communication.

Course Features

Advanced FunctionsPolynomial, rational, exponential, logarithmic, inverse, and composite functions are analyzed across representations.
Trigonometric ReasoningUnit-circle relationships, identities, equations, graphs, and applications develop fluent trigonometric thinking.
Vectors & ModellingVectors, polar coordinates, parametric representations, and contextual models connect algebra and geometry.
Calculus ReadinessRates of change, function behavior, limits preparation, and mathematical precision prepare students for Calculus I.
