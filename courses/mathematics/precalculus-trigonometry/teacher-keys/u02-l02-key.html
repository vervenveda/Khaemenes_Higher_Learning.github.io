Zeros, Factors & Multiplicity

U02 · L02
1

State the end behavior of f(x)=−3x⁴+2x.

Answer: Both ends decrease toward −∞
Reasoning: Even degree with negative leading coefficient.
2

Find the zeros of (x−2)²(x+5).

Answer: x=2 (multiplicity 2), x=−5
Reasoning: Set each factor equal to zero.
3

How does the graph behave at x=2 for (x−2)²(x+5)?

Answer: It touches and turns
Reasoning: Even multiplicity produces a touch.
4

Divide x³−4x²+x+6 by x−2.

Answer: x²−2x−3
Reasoning: Synthetic division with 2 gives coefficients 1,−2,−3 and remainder 0.
5

Find the remainder when f(x)=x³+2x−1 is divided by x−3.

Answer: 32
Reasoning: Remainder theorem: f(3)=27+6−1=32.
6

What is the maximum possible number of turning points of a degree-6 polynomial?

Answer: 5
Reasoning: A degree n polynomial has at most n−1 turning points.
