Exponential and Logarithmic Equations

U03 · L05
1

Rewrite log₂32=5 in exponential form.

Answer: 2⁵=32
Reasoning: A logarithm states the exponent.
2

Evaluate log₁₀0.001.

Answer: −3
Reasoning: 10^{-3}=0.001.
3

Expand ln(3x²).

Answer: ln3+2ln|x|
Reasoning: Use product and power properties.
4

Condense 2lnx−ln(x+1).

Answer: ln(x²/(x+1))
Reasoning: Use power and quotient properties.
5

Solve log₃(x−1)=2.

Answer: x=10
Reasoning: x−1=9.
6

Use change of base to express log₇20.

Answer: ln20/ln7
Reasoning: Change-of-base formula.
