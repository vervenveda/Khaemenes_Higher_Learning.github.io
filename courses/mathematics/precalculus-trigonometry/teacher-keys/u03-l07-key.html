Exponential Regression and Model Validation

U03 · L07
1

A quantity begins at 500 and grows 4% yearly. Write the model.

Answer: A=500(1.04)^t
Reasoning: Growth factor is 1+0.04.
2

Evaluate 200(0.8)^3.

Answer: 102.4
Reasoning: Multiply 200 by 0.512.
3

A population doubles every 6 years. Give a base-2 model.

Answer: P=P₀·2^{t/6}
Reasoning: The exponent counts six-year doubling intervals.
4

Solve 3^x=81.

Answer: x=4
Reasoning: 81=3⁴.
5

Find the continuous growth rate k if A=A₀e^{0.07t}.

Answer: 7% per time unit
Reasoning: k=0.07 corresponds to a continuous rate of 7%.
6

Find the half-life for A=A₀e^{-0.2t}.

Answer: ln2/0.2≈3.47
Reasoning: Set e^{-0.2t}=1/2 and solve.
