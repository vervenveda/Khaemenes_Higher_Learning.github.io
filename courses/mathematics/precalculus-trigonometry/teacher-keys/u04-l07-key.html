Circular Motion and Periodic Position

U04 · L07
1

Convert 150° to radians.

Answer: 5π/6
Reasoning: Multiply by π/180.
2

Convert 7π/4 to degrees.

Answer: 315°
Reasoning: Multiply by 180/π.
3

Find an angle coterminal with −π/3 in [0,2π).

Answer: 5π/3
Reasoning: Add 2π.
4

Find sin(5π/6).

Answer: 1/2
Reasoning: Reference angle π/6 in Quadrant II.
5

Find cos(7π/4).

Answer: √2/2
Reasoning: Reference angle π/4 in Quadrant IV; cosine is positive.
6

A wheel of radius 4 turns through 3π/2 radians. Find arc length.

Answer: 6π
Reasoning: s=rθ=4(3π/2).
