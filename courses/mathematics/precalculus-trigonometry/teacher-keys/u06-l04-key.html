Double-Angle and Half-Angle Formulas

U06 · L04
1

Rewrite tan x using sine and cosine.

Answer: sin x/cos x
Reasoning: Quotient identity.
2

Simplify 1−sin²x.

Answer: cos²x
Reasoning: Pythagorean identity.
3

Simplify sec²x−tan²x.

Answer: 1
Reasoning: Rearrange sec²x=1+tan²x.
4

Expand sin(a+b).

Answer: sin a cos b+cos a sin b
Reasoning: Sine angle-sum identity.
5

Write cos(2x) in terms of cosine only.

Answer: 2cos²x−1
Reasoning: Double-angle identity.
6

Evaluate sin75° exactly.

Answer: (√6+√2)/4
Reasoning: Use sin(45°+30°).
