Solving Basic Trigonometric Equations

U06 · L06
1

Solve sin x=1/2 on [0,2π).

Answer: x=π/6,5π/6
Reasoning: Sine is positive in Quadrants I and II.
2

Solve cos x=−√2/2 on [0,2π).

Answer: x=3π/4,5π/4
Reasoning: Cosine is negative in Quadrants II and III.
3

Solve tan x=1 on [0,2π).

Answer: x=π/4,5π/4
Reasoning: Tangent has period π.
4

Solve 2sin x−1=0 on [0,2π).

Answer: x=π/6,5π/6
Reasoning: Reduce to sin x=1/2.
5

Give the general solution of cos x=1.

Answer: x=2πn
Reasoning: Cosine equals one at full turns.
6

Why must solutions be checked after squaring an equation?

Answer: Squaring may create extraneous solutions
Reasoning: The reverse implication need not hold.
