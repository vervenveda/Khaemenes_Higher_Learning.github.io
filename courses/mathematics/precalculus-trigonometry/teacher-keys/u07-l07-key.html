U07 · L07
1

In a right triangle, opposite=6 and hypotenuse=10. Find sin θ.

Answer: 3/5
Reasoning: sin θ=opposite/hypotenuse.
2

Given a=7, A=35°, B=62°, find angle C.

Answer: 83°
Reasoning: Triangle angles sum to 180°.
3

Which law fits SAS data?

Answer: Law of Cosines
Reasoning: Two sides and included angle determine the opposite side.
4

Find c when a=5, b=8, C=60°.

Answer: 7
Reasoning: c²=25+64−80(1/2)=49.
5

Find the area with a=10, b=12, C=30°.

Answer: 30
Reasoning: K=½ab sin C.
6

A bearing of N35°E corresponds to what standard compass description?

Answer: 35° east of north
Reasoning: Read from north toward east.
