Complex Numbers in Rectangular and Polar Form

U08 · L04
1

Write 3+3i in polar form.

Answer: 3√2 cis(π/4)
Reasoning: Magnitude is 3√2 and argument is π/4.
2

Multiply 2cis(π/6) by 3cis(π/4).

Answer: 6cis(5π/12)
Reasoning: Multiply magnitudes and add angles.
3

Divide 8cis(2π/3) by 2cis(π/6).

Answer: 4cis(π/2)
Reasoning: Divide magnitudes and subtract angles.
4

Use De Moivre to square 2cis(π/3).

Answer: 4cis(2π/3)
Reasoning: Square magnitude and double angle.
5

How many distinct complex fourth roots does a nonzero number have?

Answer: 4
Reasoning: The roots are equally spaced around a circle.
6

What geometric action is multiplication by i?

Answer: Rotate 90° counterclockwise
Reasoning: i has magnitude 1 and argument π/2.
