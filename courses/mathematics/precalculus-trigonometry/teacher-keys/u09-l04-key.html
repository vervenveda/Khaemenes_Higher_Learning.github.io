Dot Product, Angle & Orthogonality

U09 · L04
1

Find the magnitude of ⟨3,4⟩.

Answer: 5
Reasoning: Use √(3²+4²).
2

Write a vector of magnitude 10 at 30° in component form.

Answer: ⟨5√3,5⟩
Reasoning: Use ⟨r cosθ,r sinθ⟩.
3

Add ⟨2,−1⟩ and ⟨5,4⟩.

Answer: ⟨7,3⟩
Reasoning: Add corresponding components.
4

Compute ⟨1,2⟩·⟨3,−4⟩.

Answer: −5
Reasoning: 1·3+2·(−4)=−5.
5

Are ⟨2,1⟩ and ⟨1,−2⟩ orthogonal?

Answer: Yes
Reasoning: Their dot product is 0.
6

Find the unit vector in the direction of ⟨6,8⟩.

Answer: ⟨3/5,4/5⟩
Reasoning: Divide by magnitude 10.
