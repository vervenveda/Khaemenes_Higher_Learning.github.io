Find the next term: 4,9,14,19,…

Answer: 24
Reasoning: Common difference is 5.
2

Write an explicit rule for 4,9,14,…

Answer: a_n=4+5(n−1)
Reasoning: Arithmetic sequence.
3

Find a_8 for a_n=3·2^{n−1}.

Answer: 384
Reasoning: 3·2⁷=384.
4

Give a recursive rule for 10,7,4,1,…

Answer: a₁=10; a_n=a_{n−1}−3
Reasoning: Subtract 3 each step.
5

Is 2,6,18,54 arithmetic or geometric?

Answer: Geometric
Reasoning: Common ratio is 3.
6

Find the 20th term of a_n=7+4(n−1).

Answer: 83
Reasoning: 7+4·19=83.
