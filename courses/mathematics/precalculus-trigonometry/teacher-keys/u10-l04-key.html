Infinite Geometric Series and Convergence

U10 · L04
1

Find 3+7+11+15+19.

Answer: 55
Reasoning: Five-term arithmetic sum.
2

Find the sum of the first 8 terms of 2,6,18,…

Answer: 6560
Reasoning: Use S_n=a₁(r^n−1)/(r−1).
3

Does 12+6+3+… converge?

Answer: Yes, to 24
Reasoning: r=1/2, so S∞=12/(1−1/2).
4

Evaluate Σ_{k=1}^{4} k².

Answer: 30
Reasoning: 1+4+9+16=30.
5

Find the coefficient of x² in (1+x)^5.

Answer: 10
Reasoning: Binomial coefficient C(5,2)=10.
6

State the convergence condition for an infinite geometric series.

Answer: |r|<1
Reasoning: Only then do terms shrink to zero fast enough.
