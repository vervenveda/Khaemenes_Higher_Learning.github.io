Matrix Transformations and Composition

U11 · L02
1

State the dimensions of [[1,2,3],[4,5,6]].

Answer: 2×3
Reasoning: Two rows and three columns.
2

Add [[1,2],[3,4]] and [[5,6],[7,8]].

Answer: [[6,8],[10,12]]
Reasoning: Add corresponding entries.
3

Can a 2×3 matrix multiply a 2×2 matrix on the right?

Answer: No
Reasoning: Inner dimensions 3 and 2 do not match.
4

Compute [[1,0],[0,−1]]⟨3,4⟩.

Answer: ⟨3,−4⟩
Reasoning: This reflects across the x-axis.
5

What matrix represents a 90° counterclockwise rotation?

Answer: [[0,−1],[1,0]]
Reasoning: It maps ⟨x,y⟩ to ⟨−y,x⟩.
6

Why does order matter in matrix transformations?

Answer: Composition is generally noncommutative
Reasoning: AB and BA apply transformations in different orders.
