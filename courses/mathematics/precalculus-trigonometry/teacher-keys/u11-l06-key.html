Hyperbolas and Asymptotic Structure

U11 · L06
1

Identify (x−2)²=8(y+1).

Answer: Parabola opening upward
Reasoning: 4p=8>0 with vertical axis.
2

Find the center of (x+3)²/16+(y−2)²/9=1.

Answer: (−3,2)
Reasoning: Read h and k from translated form.
3

Find the semi-major axis of x²/25+y²/9=1.

Answer: 5
Reasoning: The larger denominator is 25.
4

Identify x²/9−y²/4=1.

Answer: Horizontal hyperbola
Reasoning: Opposite signs and positive x-term.
5

State the asymptotes of x²/9−y²/4=1.

Answer: y=±(2/3)x
Reasoning: Use y=±(b/a)x.
6

For y²=12x, find p.

Answer: 3
Reasoning: 4p=12.
