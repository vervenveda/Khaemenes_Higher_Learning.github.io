Continuity, Discontinuity & End Behavior

U12 · L05
1

Find the average rate of change of x² from 2 to 5.

Answer: 7
Reasoning: (25−4)/(5−2)=7.
2

Simplify [(x+h)²−x²]/h.

Answer: 2x+h
Reasoning: Expand and cancel h.
3

Evaluate lim_{x→3}(2x+1).

Answer: 7
Reasoning: Polynomials are continuous.
4

Evaluate lim_{x→2}(x²−4)/(x−2).

Answer: 4
Reasoning: Factor to x+2 for x≠2.
5

Can a two-sided limit exist if one-sided limits differ?

Answer: No
Reasoning: Both one-sided limits must agree.
6

Name the discontinuity in (x²−1)/(x−1) at x=1.

Answer: Removable
Reasoning: The factor cancels but the original function is undefined there.
