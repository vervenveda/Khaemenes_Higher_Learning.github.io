Choosing, Fitting & Validating Models

Analyze functions through algebraic, graphical, numerical, verbal, and contextual representations, then defend what each representation reveals. In this lesson, the central focus is choosing, fitting & validating models. Learners move among symbolic, graphical, numerical, and contextual evidence rather than relying on one representation alone.

Learning intentions

  • Interpret the defining quantities and notation used in choosing, fitting & validating models.
  • Construct or analyze at least two representations related to choosing, fitting & validating models.
  • Show symbolic work with correct domains, units, intervals, or angle measures.
  • Explain why the selected method is valid and check the result against another representation.

Essential question

Worked reasoning

Representation checkEvaluate the function numerically, locate the same information on its graph, and explain the contextual meaning.
Model choiceChoose a function family from its pattern of change, then verify residuals and state the valid domain.

Misconception check: A graph is not merely a picture; its scale, domain, intercepts, rates, and end behavior are mathematical evidence.

Communication and reasoning

Write a complete response that identifies the representation used, shows the symbolic steps, interprets parameters or units, and verifies the conclusion with a second representation or reasonableness check.
