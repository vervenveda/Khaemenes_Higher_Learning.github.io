Function Atlas & Model Selection Defense

Function Atlas & Model Selection Defense

How do domain, range, rate of change, composition, inverse structure, and multiple representations reveal what a function can model?

  1. Define a focused mathematical question and the quantities involved.
  2. Create at least three representations: symbolic, graphical, numerical, geometric, or contextual.
  3. Use the scientific calculator, CAD, or an Arcade studio only when it strengthens evidence.
  4. Validate results with a second method, residuals, a special case, or a reasonableness check.
  5. State assumptions, limitations, precision, and the valid domain.
  6. Prepare a one-page written summary and a three-minute oral defense.
