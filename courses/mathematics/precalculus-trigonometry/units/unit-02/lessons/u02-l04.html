Polynomial Division & the Remainder Theorem

Connect degree, leading coefficient, zeros, factors, multiplicity, finite differences, and end behavior to polynomial structure. In this lesson, the central focus is polynomial division & the remainder theorem. Learners move among symbolic, graphical, numerical, and contextual evidence rather than relying on one representation alone.

Open-AgePrintableFoundation · Core · ExtendedMastery target 80%

Learning intentions

  • Interpret the defining quantities and notation used in polynomial division & the remainder theorem.
  • Construct or analyze at least two representations related to polynomial division & the remainder theorem.
  • Show symbolic work with correct domains, units, intervals, or angle measures.
  • Explain why the selected method is valid and check the result against another representation.

Essential question

How do algebraic structure, zeros, multiplicity, asymptotes, and rates of change determine polynomial and rational behavior?

Worked reasoning

Structure to graphFactor first to identify zeros and multiplicities, then combine end behavior with selected points.
Domain auditRecord restrictions from the original denominator before simplifying so holes and asymptotes are classified correctly.

Misconception check: A zero with even multiplicity touches the axis; a zero with odd multiplicity crosses it.
