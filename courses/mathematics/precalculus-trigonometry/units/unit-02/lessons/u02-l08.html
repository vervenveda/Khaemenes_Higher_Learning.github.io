Worked reasoning

Structure to graphFactor first to identify zeros and multiplicities, then combine end behavior with selected points.
Domain auditRecord restrictions from the original denominator before simplifying so holes and asymptotes are classified correctly.

Misconception check: A canceled factor creates a hole, not a vertical asymptote, because the original domain restriction remains.
