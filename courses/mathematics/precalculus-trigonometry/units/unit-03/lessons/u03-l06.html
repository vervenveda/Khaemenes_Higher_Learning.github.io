Logarithmic Scales and Data Interpretation

Use logarithms as inverse functions and exponents to solve equations, compare scales, and linearize multiplicative relationships. In this lesson, the central focus is logarithmic scales and data interpretation. Learners move among symbolic, graphical, numerical, and contextual evidence rather than relying on one representation alone.

Essential question

How do multiplicative change, inverse structure, logarithmic scales, and parameter interpretation support defensible growth and decay models?

Multiple-representation model

Use logarithms as inverse functions and exponents to solve equations, compare scales, and linearize multiplicative relationships.

\log_b(x)=y\Longleftrightarrow b^y=x\log_b(MN)=\log_bM+\log_bN\log_bM=\frac{\ln M}{\ln b}

Worked reasoning

Growth modelA 6% annual increase is modeled by A=A₀(1.06)^t, not A=A₀+0.06t.
Inverse reasoningTo solve 5e^{0.3t}=42, isolate the exponential and apply ln: t=ln(42/5)/0.3.

Misconception check: Logarithms distribute over products and quotients, not over sums and differences.
