Essential question

How does circular measure connect angles, coordinates, arc length, angular velocity, and exact trigonometric values?

Multiple-representation model

Measure rotation in radians and use the unit circle to connect angle, coordinate, arc length, angular velocity, and exact trigonometric values.

s=r\thetaA=\frac12r^2\theta(x,y)=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)\omega=\frac{\theta}{t}

Worked reasoning

Exact circular valueFor θ=5π/6, the reference angle is π/6 and the point lies in Quadrant II, so cos θ=−√3/2 and sin θ=1/2.
Arc modelWith r=9 and θ=2π/3, arc length is s=rθ=6π and sector area is ½r²θ=27π.

Misconception check: Radians are dimensionless ratios of arc length to radius; they are not written with a degree symbol.
