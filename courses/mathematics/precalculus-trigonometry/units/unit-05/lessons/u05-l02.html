Worked reasoning

Parameter readingFor y=3 sin(2(x−π/4))+1, amplitude is 3, period is π, phase shift is π/4 right, and midline is y=1.
Model defenseIdentify max, min, and one full cycle first; then verify that the proposed equation reaches the observed extrema at the correct input values.

Misconception check: The coefficient B changes the period reciprocally; the period is not simply B.
