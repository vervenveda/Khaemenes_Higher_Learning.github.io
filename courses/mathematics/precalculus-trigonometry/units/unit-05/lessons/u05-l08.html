Essential question

How do amplitude, period, phase, midline, and reciprocal behavior describe and model repeating phenomena?

Worked reasoning

Nearby behaviorIf a graph approaches 4 from both sides as x→2, then the limit is 4 even when f(2) is undefined.
Difference quotientFor f(x)=x², [f(a+h)−f(a)]/h=2a+h, which approaches 2a as h approaches 0.

Misconception check: A limit describes nearby behavior and may exist even when the function value is missing or different.
