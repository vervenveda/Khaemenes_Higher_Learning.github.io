General Solutions, Technology & Extraneous Results

Learners move among symbolic, graphical, numerical, and contextual evidence rather than relying on one representation alone.

Worked reasoning

Identity strategyRewrite tan x as sin x/cos x and use sin²x+cos²x=1 only after the expression has a common denominator.
Equation strategySolve the reference equation, locate every valid quadrant, then add periodic solutions and test the original domain.

Misconception check: An inverse-trigonometric output gives one principal value, not every solution to the periodic equation.

Communication and reasoning
