Essential question

How do recursive and explicit structures describe discrete change, accumulation, convergence, and financial or scientific processes?

Multiple-representation model

Analyze sums with sigma notation, geometric convergence, binomial structure, and discrete financial or dynamical models.

\sum_{k=1}^{n}a_kS_n=a_1\frac{1-r^n}{1-r}S_\infty=\frac{a_1}{1-r},\ |r|<1

Worked reasoning

Explicit ruleAn arithmetic sequence 7,12,17,… has a_n=7+5(n−1).
ConvergenceFor 18+6+2+…, r=1/3, so S∞=18/(1−1/3)=27.

Misconception check: An infinite geometric series converges only when the absolute value of the common ratio is less than one.
