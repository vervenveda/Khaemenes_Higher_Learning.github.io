Worked reasoning

Dimension checkA 2×3 matrix can multiply a 3×1 vector, producing a 2×1 vector.
CompositionIf A is applied first and B second, the combined column-vector transformation is BA, not AB.

Misconception check: Matrix multiplication is generally not commutative; AB and BA can represent different compositions or may not both exist.
