Defining a Researchable Modelling Question

In this lesson, the central focus is defining a researchable modelling question. Learners move among symbolic, graphical, numerical, and contextual evidence rather than relying on one representation alone.

Worked reasoning

Residual checkA positive residual means the observed value is above the model prediction.
Model limitationA model fitted on 0≤t≤12 should not be extrapolated far beyond 12 without a contextual argument and new validation data.

Misconception check: A high numerical fit does not prove causation or guarantee valid extrapolation beyond the observed domain.

Communication and reasoning

Write a complete response that identifies the representation used, shows the symbolic steps, interprets parameters or units, and verifies the conclusion with a second representation or reasonableness check.
