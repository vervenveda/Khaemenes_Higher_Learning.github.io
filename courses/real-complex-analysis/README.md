# Khaemenes Higher Learning Real & Complex Analysis

A rigorous advanced sequence in completeness, sequences and series, metric spaces, continuity, differentiation, integration, function convergence, measure and Lebesgue integration, holomorphic functions, Cauchy theory, residues, conformal mapping, and research-level exposition.

- 36 weeks · 180 sessions
- 13 units · 92 lessons · 92 faculty keys
- 13 investigations · 13 mastery checks
- 40-question diagnostic · 60-question midterm · 100-question final
- Three pathways and three interactive laboratories

**Curriculum author:** Jennifer Pearl
