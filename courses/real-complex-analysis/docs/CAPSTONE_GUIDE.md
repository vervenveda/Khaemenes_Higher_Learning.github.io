# Advanced Research Capstone Guide

## Required components

1. Feasible theorem-level or analysis research question
2. Definitions, notation, and prerequisite theorem map
3. Verified literature and source architecture
4. Examples, nonexamples, computation, or visual exploration clearly labelled as evidence
5. Main theorem sequence or analytical result with rigorous proof
6. Counterexample and hypothesis stress test
7. Reproducible appendix and disclosed tools
8. Limitations and open questions
9. Journal-style exposition
10. Oral defense and response to critique

The capstone is evaluated for correctness, mathematical maturity, exposition, source integrity, reproducibility, and the learner’s ability to defend every submitted claim.
