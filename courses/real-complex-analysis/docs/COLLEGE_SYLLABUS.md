# Khaemenes Higher Learning Real & Complex Analysis — Advanced Syllabus

**Length:** 36 weeks · 180 sessions  
**Lessons:** 92 interactive lessons and 92 faculty keys  
**Author:** Jennifer Pearl

## Academic level

An advanced upper-level and graduate-preparatory sequence with selected beginning-graduate topics. It is not represented as a replacement for a university graduate qualifying sequence.

## Prerequisite

A completed Introduction to Proofs course or equivalent proof fluency, single-variable calculus, and mathematical maturity. Linear algebra is strongly recommended.

## Course outcomes

- Prove foundational results about real numbers, sequences, series, metric spaces, continuity, differentiation, and integration.
- Analyze pointwise and uniform convergence and justify interchange of limiting operations.
- Use measure and Lebesgue integration theorems with verified hypotheses.
- Develop complex differentiability, Cauchy theory, Laurent series, residues, harmonic functions, and conformal mapping.
- Write and defend advanced theorem sequences with accurate sourcing, limitations, and research extensions.

## Unit sequence

1. **Proof Foundations & the Real Number System** — Weeks 1–2
2. **Sequences, Subsequences & Completeness** — Weeks 3–5
3. **Infinite Series & Power Series** — Weeks 6–8
4. **Metric Spaces, Topology & Compactness** — Weeks 9–11
5. **Continuity, Uniform Continuity & Function Spaces** — Weeks 12–14
6. **Differentiation, Mean-Value Theorems & Taylor Theory** — Weeks 15–17
7. **Riemann Integration & the Fundamental Theorem** — Weeks 18–20
8. **Sequences & Series of Functions** — Weeks 21–23
9. **Measure, Lebesgue Integration & Lp Foundations** — Weeks 24–26
10. **Complex Numbers, Analyticity & Conformal Geometry** — Weeks 27–29
11. **Complex Integration & Cauchy Theory** — Weeks 30–31
12. **Singularities, Laurent Series & Residues** — Weeks 32–34
13. **Harmonic, Conformal & Research-Level Complex Analysis** — Weeks 35–36

## Evidence model

The course includes 13 investigations, 13 mastery checks, a 40-question diagnostic, 60-question midterm, 100-question final, portfolio, independent research capstone, reproducible appendix, and oral defense. Corrections and proof revision are required components of mastery.

## Technology and integrity

Software, calculators, proof assistants, plotting, numerical experiments, and AI may support exploration when disclosed. Learners remain responsible for verifying every theorem, source, proof, calculation, and citation and must be able to defend submitted work independently.
