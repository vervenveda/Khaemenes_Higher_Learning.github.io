# Course Outcomes

- Prove foundational results about real numbers, sequences, series, metric spaces, continuity, differentiation, and integration.
- Analyze pointwise and uniform convergence and justify interchange of limiting operations.
- Use measure and Lebesgue integration theorems with verified hypotheses.
- Develop complex differentiability, Cauchy theory, Laurent series, residues, harmonic functions, and conformal mapping.
- Write and defend advanced theorem sequences with accurate sourcing, limitations, and research extensions.
