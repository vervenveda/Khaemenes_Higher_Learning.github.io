# Standards and Curriculum Union

This curriculum unifies advanced proof-writing, upper-level analysis, research communication, accessibility, reproducibility, and responsible technology use. It is informed by established university course topic sequences but uses original Khaemenes lessons, questions, investigations, assessments, keys, laboratories, and records.

## Structural expectations

- precise definitions and quantified statements
- complete theorem hypotheses and proof dependencies
- counterexamples and boundary cases
- multiple representations with explicit limitations
- mathematical writing, source verification, peer review, and oral defense
- local-first records, printable evidence, and accessible interaction
