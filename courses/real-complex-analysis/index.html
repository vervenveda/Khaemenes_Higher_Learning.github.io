About Real & Complex Analysis

Real & Complex Analysis develops rigorous understanding of limits, convergence, continuity, integration, and analytic functions. The course connects foundational real analysis with the geometric and computational power of complex-variable methods.

Course Features

Real AnalysisSequences, limits, continuity, differentiation, convergence, and rigorous argument deepen foundations from calculus.
Metric & Function SpacesMetric spaces, compactness, completeness, uniform convergence, and function sequences extend analytical structure.
Complex AnalysisComplex differentiation, analytic functions, power series, contour integration, and conformal ideas develop complex-variable reasoning.
Residues & MappingResidue methods, contour techniques, singularities, and mapping theory connect complex structure with computation and geometry.
