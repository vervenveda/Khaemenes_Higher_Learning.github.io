# Khaemenes Higher Learning Science Department

**Canonical department:** `/courses/science/`

This is the canonical collegiate Science Department. It is intentionally organized to the same structural standard as the advanced Mathematics courses.

## Course-package parity standard

Every completed collegiate science course should provide the same major package classes used by advanced Mathematics:

- `index.html`
- `course-data.js`
- `course-map.json`
- `manifest.webmanifest`
- `service-worker.js`
- `offline.html`
- `README.md`
- `FILE_MANIFEST.md`
- `PROFILE_INTEGRATION_NOTES.md`
- `UPDATE_NOTES.md`
- `UPLOAD_MAP.md`
- `VALIDATION_REPORT.md`
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`
- `diagnostic/`
- `units/`
- `assessments/`
- `records/`
- `teacher/`
- `teacher-keys/`
- `tools/`
- `docs/`
- `assets/`

Science courses additionally require authentic laboratory/investigation evidence, quantitative uncertainty/error analysis, discipline-specific vocabulary, formal scientific communication, and student-owned research records.

## 100-level pathways

- BIO 102 — General Biology II

### Chemistry
- CHEM 101 — General Chemistry I
- CHEM 102 — General Chemistry II

### Physics
- PHYS 101 — College Physics I
- PHYS 102 — College Physics II
- PHYS 151 — University Physics I
- PHYS 152 — University Physics II

### Earth, Environment & Space
- ENV 101 — Earth & Environmental Science
- ASTR 101 — Astronomy & Space Science

## High-school bridge

- Biology & Biotechnology 10 → BIO 101
- Chemistry & Materials Science 11 → CHEM 101
- Physics & Engineering 12 → PHYS 101 or PHYS 151
- Integrated Science 9 → broad preparation for all introductory pathways
