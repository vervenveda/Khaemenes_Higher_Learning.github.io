# BIO 101 — General Biology I

**Khaemenes Higher Learning Science Department**  
**Status:** Foundation architecture complete · individual lesson writing pending

BIO 101 is organized to the same full-course structural standard used by Advanced Mathematics.

## Locked course foundation

- 36 weeks
- 180 sessions
- 13 units
- 92 planned lessons
- 13 evidence-bearing investigations
- 40-question readiness diagnostic
- 13 randomized unit mastery checks at 80%
- 60-question cumulative midterm
- 100-question comprehensive final
- original assessment-bank target of at least 220 items
- 216 vocabulary placements across all 36 weeks
- laboratory portfolio
- independent research capstone and oral defense
- Foundation · Core · Honors / Extended pathways

## Academic status

The architecture is source-validated, but the course is **not yet instructionally complete**. Individual lessons, faculty keys, full assessment banks, laboratory pages, learner records, offline package, and live-browser certification still need to be built.

BIO 101 is designed as a rigorous first-semester college biology course. Institutional credit, transfer, and placement decisions remain with the receiving institution.
