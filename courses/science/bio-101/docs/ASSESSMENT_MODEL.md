# BIO 101 Assessment Model

- 40-question readiness diagnostic — support/placement only, not official mastery
- 13 randomized 10-question unit mastery checks — 80% required
- 13 laboratory/investigation submissions with evidence review
- original assessment bank target: at least 220 items across concept, evidence, quantitative, model, and application reasoning
- 60-question cumulative midterm — closed book — 80% mastery
- 100-question comprehensive final — closed book — 80% mastery
- corrected-work portfolio
- Unit 13 independent research capstone and oral defense

## Recommended weighting
Coursework 20% · Unit mastery 20% · Laboratory investigations 25% · Midterm 10% · Final 15% · Capstone 10%.

## Retake and integrity rule
A submitted mastery form locks its responses. A retake must generate a fresh randomized form from the relevant bank. Protected midterm/final content must never be exposed through public review pages.
