# BIO 101 Curriculum Map

**Course:** Khaemenes Higher Learning General Biology I  
**Catalog:** BIO 101  
**Length:** 36 weeks · 180 sessions · 92 planned lessons  
**Mastery target:** 80% on formal mastery assessments  
**Pathways:** Foundation · Core · Honors / Extended  
**Status:** Architecture locked before lesson writing

## Unit 01 · College Biology Readiness, Safety & Scientific Reasoning — Weeks 1, 2
How do safe practice, quantitative reasoning, uncertainty, experimental design, and scientific argument establish trustworthy biological evidence?

- 01. Laboratory Safety, Risk & Responsible Investigation
- 02. Variables, Controls & Experimental Logic
- 03. Measurement, Units & Biological Uncertainty
- 04. Graph Choice, Data Displays & Quantitative Claims
- 05. Replication, Variation & Reproducibility
- 06. CER, Alternative Explanations & Limitations
- 07. Readiness Synthesis & Evidence Defense

**Investigation:** Readiness Investigation & Evidence Defense

## Unit 02 · Molecular Foundations of Life — Weeks 3, 4, 5
How do water, carbon chemistry, macromolecular structure, and molecular interactions constrain biological function?

- 01. Water, Polarity & Hydrogen Bonding
- 02. Carbon Skeletons & Functional Groups
- 03. Carbohydrates & Lipids
- 04. Proteins, Folding & Molecular Function
- 05. Nucleic Acids & Information Molecules
- 06. pH, Buffers & Biological Conditions
- 07. Molecular Structure–Function Investigation

**Investigation:** Structure–Function Molecular Evidence Laboratory

## Unit 03 · Cell Structure, Membranes & Microscopy — Weeks 6, 7, 8
How do cellular structures, membranes, transport processes, and microscopy evidence explain organization and exchange in living systems?

- 01. Cell Theory & Evidence Across Scales
- 02. Prokaryotic and Eukaryotic Organization
- 03. Organelles & Compartmentalization
- 04. Membrane Structure & Selective Permeability
- 05. Diffusion, Osmosis & Water Potential Reasoning
- 06. Active Transport & Membrane Gradients
- 07. Microscopy, Scale & Membrane Evidence Investigation

**Investigation:** Microscopy, Membrane Transport & Scale Laboratory

## Unit 04 · Enzymes, Metabolism & Bioenergetics — Weeks 9, 10, 11
How do enzymes, coupled reactions, ATP, and metabolic pathways control the rate and direction of cellular chemistry?

- 01. Enzymes & Activation-Energy Barriers
- 02. Active Sites, Specificity & Molecular Conditions
- 03. Reaction Rates, Temperature, pH & Concentration
- 04. ATP, Energy Coupling & Cellular Work
- 05. Oxidation–Reduction & Electron Transfer
- 06. Metabolic Pathways & Regulatory Logic
- 07. Enzyme Kinetics Investigation & Model Defense

**Investigation:** Enzyme Kinetics & Metabolic Rate Laboratory

## Unit 05 · Photosynthesis, Respiration & Energy Transformation — Weeks 12, 13, 14
How do photosynthesis and cellular respiration transform matter and energy while obeying conservation and system constraints?

- 01. Photosynthetic Pigments & Light Capture
- 02. Light Reactions & Chemiosmosis
- 03. Carbon Fixation & Biomass
- 04. Glycolysis & Pyruvate Processing
- 05. Citric Acid Cycle & Oxidative Phosphorylation
- 06. Fermentation, Oxygen Limits & Metabolic Flexibility
- 07. Energy Transformation Investigation & Carbon Accounting

**Investigation:** Cellular Energy & Gas-Exchange Investigation

## Unit 06 · Cell Communication, Homeostasis & Cell Cycle — Weeks 15, 16, 17
How do signaling networks, feedback systems, and cell-cycle controls coordinate multicellular life and constrain disease mechanisms?

- 01. Signals, Receptors & Specificity
- 02. Signal Transduction & Cellular Response
- 03. Negative Feedback & Homeostasis
- 04. Positive Feedback & Biological Amplification
- 05. Cell Cycle, Mitosis & Checkpoints
- 06. Apoptosis, Mutation & Cancer Models
- 07. Systems Regulation Investigation & Model Limits

**Investigation:** Signaling, Feedback & Cell-Cycle Model Investigation

## Unit 07 · Meiosis, Mendelian Genetics & Quantitative Inheritance — Weeks 18, 19, 20
How do meiosis, segregation, probability, and statistical evidence explain inheritance while preserving uncertainty in biological prediction?

- 01. Midterm Synthesis, Corrections & Genetics Bridge
- 02. Meiosis & Chromosome Behavior
- 03. Crossing Over & Independent Assortment
- 04. Alleles, Genotypes & Phenotypes
- 05. Probability Models & Punnett Reasoning
- 06. Pedigrees, Sex Linkage & Statistical Evidence
- 07. Inheritance Investigation & Chi-Square Reasoning

**Investigation:** Inheritance Probability & Pedigree Evidence Laboratory

## Unit 08 · DNA Replication, Gene Expression & Regulation — Weeks 21, 22, 23
How does molecular information move from DNA replication through gene expression, and how is that flow regulated in cells?

- 01. DNA Structure, Antiparallel Strands & Base Pairing
- 02. Semiconservative DNA Replication
- 03. Transcription & RNA Processing
- 04. Translation, Codons & Protein Synthesis
- 05. Mutation Types & Molecular Consequences
- 06. Promoters, Transcription Factors & Regulatory Networks
- 07. Gene Expression Investigation & Evidence Defense

**Investigation:** Gene Expression & Regulation Evidence Laboratory

## Unit 09 · Genomics, Biotechnology & Bioethics — Weeks 24, 25, 26
How can molecular tools measure, amplify, sequence, compare, and edit biological information, and what evidentiary and ethical limits govern their use?

- 01. PCR Logic, Primers & Amplification
- 02. Gel Electrophoresis & Molecular Separation
- 03. DNA Sequencing & Genomic Comparison
- 04. Genomics, Databases & Biological Inference
- 05. CRISPR Concepts & Gene Editing Boundaries
- 06. Consent, Privacy & Biotechnology Ethics
- 07. Biotechnology Evidence Investigation & Chain of Custody

**Investigation:** PCR, Electrophoresis & Genomic Evidence Laboratory

## Unit 10 · Evolution, Population Genetics & Phylogeny — Weeks 27, 28, 29
How do mutation, selection, drift, gene flow, and multiple evidence streams explain population change and common ancestry?

- 01. Lines of Evidence for Evolutionary Change
- 02. Allele Frequencies & Population Variation

- 04. Genetic Drift, Bottlenecks & Founder Effects
- 05. Gene Flow, Mutation & Population Structure
- 06. Speciation & Reproductive Isolation
- 07. Phylogenetic Analysis & Evidence Limits

**Investigation:** Population Change & Phylogenetic Evidence Laboratory

## Unit 11 · Ecology, Population Dynamics & Biogeochemical Systems — Weeks 30, 31, 32
How do energy flow, population regulation, species interactions, and biogeochemical cycles produce ecosystem patterns and resilience?

- 01. Energy Flow, Trophic Structure & Productivity
- 02. Exponential and Logistic Population Models
- 03. Competition, Predation & Community Interactions
- 04. Succession, Biodiversity & Resilience
- 05. Carbon Cycling & Reservoirs
- 06. Nitrogen Cycling, Nutrients & Human Impacts
- 07. Ecosystem Data Investigation & Systems Model

**Investigation:** Ecosystem Systems & Population Data Laboratory

## Unit 12 · Physiology, Systems Integration & Comparative Biology — Weeks 33, 34
How do cells, tissues, organs, and feedback networks integrate transport, exchange, signaling, and regulation across organisms?

- 01. Tissues, Organs & Functional Integration
- 02. Transport Systems & Exchange Surfaces
- 03. Gas Exchange & Oxygen Transport
- 04. Osmoregulation & Water Balance
- 05. Neural and Endocrine Coordination
- 06. Immune Defense & Biological Recognition
- 07. Comparative Physiology Investigation & Systems Defense

**Investigation:** Comparative Physiology & Homeostatic Systems Investigation

## Unit 13 · Independent Biological Research, Reproducibility & Capstone Defense — Weeks 35, 36
How does a biologist design, execute, analyze, revise, and defend an investigation whose claims remain proportional to the evidence?

- 03. Experimental Design, Controls & Replication
- 04. Data Analysis, Effect Size & Uncertainty
- 05. Reproducibility, Null Results & Revision
- 06. Scientific Figures, Abstracts & Formal Reporting
- 07. Peer Review, Limitations & Research Ethics
- 08. Capstone Portfolio, Oral Defense & BIO 102 Bridge

**Investigation:** BIO 101 Independent Research Capstone & Oral Defense
