# Statistics Capstone Guide

The capstone must include a feasible investigative question; population and cases; variables and operational definitions; data source or collection design; ethics and privacy review; cleaning and provenance log; exploratory displays; justified statistical methods; assumptions and diagnostics; uncertainty and effect sizes; sensitivity or resampling analysis; limitations; reproducible files; a technical report; and a 6–10 minute oral defense. Causal claims require randomized or otherwise compelling design evidence.
