# Khaemenes Higher Learning Statistics

**Course code:** KH-HL-MATH-STAT1  
**Length:** 36 weeks · 180 sessions  
**Prerequisite:** Algebra I; Algebra II recommended. Calculus is not required.  
**Author:** Jennifer Pearl

## Course description

A rigorous introductory college statistics course emphasizing the statistical problem-solving process, data ethics, exploratory analysis, study design, probability, random variables, sampling distributions, estimation, hypothesis testing, regression, categorical methods, resampling, Bayesian foundations, reproducibility, and an independent investigation.

## Learning architecture

Each lesson includes concept development, worked reasoning, Foundation/Core/Honors pathways, four interactive questions, a six-problem printable worksheet, reflection, and a matching faculty key. Each unit includes an investigation, pathway sets, and mastery check.

## Grading guidance

- Lessons and practice: 20%
- Unit mastery checks: 20%
- Investigations and portfolio: 20%
- Midterm: 15%
- Final: 15%
- Capstone and oral defense: 10%

## Technology and integrity

Technology may support visualization, simulation, computation, resampling, and reproducibility. Learners must still identify variables, design, assumptions, uncertainty, context, and limits. Data fabrication, hidden exclusions, undisclosed generated work, or causal claims unsupported by design do not count as mastery.
