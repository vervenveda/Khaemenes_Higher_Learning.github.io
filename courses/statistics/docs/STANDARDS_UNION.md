# Standards and Curriculum Union

The course synthesizes current introductory-college expectations reflected in the American Statistical Association GAISE recommendations, current College Board AP Statistics revisions and practices, and broad introductory statistics scope represented by OpenStax. The Khaemenes course extends the common introductory sequence through reproducibility, resampling, Bayesian foundations, multiple regression, fairness, and a substantial capstone. It is college-level and AP-informed but does not claim AP authorization.
