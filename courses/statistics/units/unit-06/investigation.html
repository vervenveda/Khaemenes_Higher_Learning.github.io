Discrete Distribution & Expected-Value Modelling Project

How do random variables and probability distributions connect uncertain outcomes to expected value, variation, and decisions?

Required evidence

  1. Investigative question and intended decision
  2. Population, sample, cases, variables, units, and data source
  3. Sampling or experimental design and ethics
  4. Exploratory displays and summaries
  5. Probability or inferential method with assumptions
  6. Uncertainty, effect size, diagnostics, and sensitivity
  7. Conclusion, limitations, and reproducible appendix

Application domain

Inventory, reliability, queues, insurance, epidemiology, finance, and operations research.

Use original or properly documented public data. Do not fabricate observations or conceal exclusions.
