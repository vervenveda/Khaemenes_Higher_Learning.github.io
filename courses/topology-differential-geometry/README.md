# Khaemenes Higher Learning Topology & Differential Geometry

A complete 36-week advanced upper-level and graduate-preparatory course in point-set topology, algebraic topology foundations, smooth manifolds, differential topology, curves and surfaces, Riemannian geometry, differential forms, bundles, global theorems and research methods.

- 13 units
- 36 weeks / 180 sessions
- 92 interactive lessons and faculty keys
- five original geometry and topology laboratories (finite topologies, polygon gluing, simplicial homology, surface curvature, and geodesics/Gauss–Bonnet)
- front-door 40-question readiness placement diagnostic; optional six-lesson Unit 00 proof and calculus bridge (60 questions, 24-question exit) before the 36-week sequence
- 13 mastery checks, midterm and final
- portfolio, research capstone and oral defense

Curriculum author: Jennifer Pearl

## Readiness placement and records
The existing 40-item diagnostic opens the new guided course entrance. A result at 80% or above routes to official Unit 01. A lower result routes to conditional Unit 00, where six prerequisite lessons require 80% and written mathematical reasoning before the 24-item exit checkpoint (20 correct meet the threshold). The diagnostic and bridge do not count against the 36 official weeks or change existing 92 lesson routes. Learners with prior records keep their evidence and receive retrospective-placement guidance instead of a forced reset. All formative results are editable browser-local self-checks, not independently authenticated credit or faculty-reviewed proofs.

### Revised placement question bank
Version `topology-readiness-v2` contains 40 distinct prerequisite checks with ten correct responses in each answer position (A–D). Older diagnostic attempts remain in local history but are not silently treated as the revised placement test; existing official course work remains preserved.
