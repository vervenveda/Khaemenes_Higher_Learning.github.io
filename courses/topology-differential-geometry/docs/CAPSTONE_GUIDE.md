# Topology & Differential Geometry Research Capstone

The capstone requires a theorem-centered investigation involving a topological space, manifold, geometric structure, invariant, or application.

## Required evidence

1. A feasible research question in topology, manifolds, differential geometry, geometric analysis, mathematical physics, computational topology, or a related field.
2. Precise definitions, notation, prerequisites, hypotheses, and a dependency map.
3. Verified literature and a transparent source architecture.
4. Examples and nonexamples, including at least one explicit space or manifold model.
5. A principal theorem sequence with complete proofs.
6. A local-to-global analysis involving charts, quotients, homotopy, homology, curvature, forms, bundles, or another invariant.
7. Reproducible diagrams or computations with parameters and independent checks.
8. A counterexample, singular case, boundary case, or hypothesis stress test.
9. Limitations, unanswered questions, and a future research direction.
10. Journal-style exposition and oral defense.

The capstone is evaluated for correctness, mathematical maturity, geometric insight, proof architecture, source integrity, reproducibility, visual clarity, and the learner’s ability to defend every claim.
