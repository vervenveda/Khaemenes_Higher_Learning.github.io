# Khaemenes Higher Learning Topology & Differential Geometry — Advanced Syllabus

**Length:** 36 weeks · 180 sessions  
**Lessons:** 92 interactive lessons and 92 faculty keys  
**Curriculum author:** Jennifer Pearl

## Academic level

An advanced upper-level and graduate-preparatory topology and differential geometry sequence with selected beginning-graduate topics. It is not represented as a replacement for a university graduate topology or geometry qualifying sequence.

## Prerequisites

Introduction to Proofs or equivalent proof fluency, Real Analysis, multivariable calculus, and Linear Algebra are required. Abstract Algebra is strongly recommended for fundamental groups, homology, and bundle theory.

## Course outcomes

- Establish proof-level fluency in point-set, algebraic, differential and geometric topology.
- Build smooth-manifold and Riemannian-geometry machinery from charts through curvature.
- Use homotopy, homology, differential forms, de Rham theory, degree, index and Euler characteristic as global invariants.
- Connect local differential data to global geometric and topological consequences.
- Conduct a reproducible theorem-centered research investigation and oral defense.

## Unit sequence

1. **Topological Spaces, Bases & Continuous Maps** — Weeks 1–3
2. **Metric Spaces, Countability & Separation** — Weeks 4–6
3. **Compactness, Connectedness, Products & Quotients** — Weeks 7–9
4. **Homotopy, Fundamental Groups & Covering Spaces** — Weeks 10–12
5. **Cell Complexes, Homology & Cohomology Foundations** — Weeks 13–15
6. **Smooth Manifolds, Charts & Tangent Spaces** — Weeks 16–18
7. **Submanifolds, Transversality & Differential Topology** — Weeks 19–21
8. **Curves, Surfaces & Classical Differential Geometry** — Weeks 22–24
9. **Riemannian Metrics, Connections & Geodesics** — Weeks 25–27
10. **Differential Forms, Integration & de Rham Theory** — Weeks 28–30
11. **Vector Bundles, Lie Groups & Connections** — Weeks 31–32
12. **Morse Theory, Global Theorems & Topological Curvature** — Weeks 33–34
13. **Geometric Topology, Applications & Research Capstone** — Weeks 35–36

## Evidence model

The course includes 13 investigations, 13 mastery checks, a 40-question diagnostic, 60-question midterm, 100-question final, portfolio, research capstone, reproducible appendix, and oral defense. Corrections and proof revision are required components of mastery.

## Technology and integrity

Software, calculators, geometry systems, plotting, numerical experiments, proof assistants, and AI may support exploration when disclosed. Learners remain responsible for verifying every definition, theorem hypothesis, proof, calculation, diagram, algorithm, and citation and must be able to defend submitted work independently.
