# Course Outcomes

Learners completing this course should be able to:

- Define and analyze topological spaces, bases, compactness, connectedness, products, quotients, homotopy, fundamental groups, coverings, and homology foundations.
- Construct smooth manifolds, charts, tangent and cotangent structures, vector fields, submanifolds, differential forms, bundles, and connections.
- Compute and interpret curve, surface and Riemannian invariants including curvature, geodesics, degree, index, Euler characteristic and de Rham classes.
- Prove local-to-global theorems with every regularity, compactness, orientation, rank, transversality and completeness hypothesis verified.
- Connect topology and differential geometry to physics, robotics, geometric computing, topological data analysis, gauge theory and global analysis.
- Design, document, reproduce and orally defend an advanced topology or geometry research project.
