# Source Register

The course architecture was calibrated against established upper-level and beginning-graduate topology and geometry sequences. All Khaemenes lessons, problems, assessments, keys, laboratories, diagrams, and prose are original.

- MIT OpenCourseWare 18.950 — Differential Geometry: rigorous curves, hypersurfaces, curvature, and lengths and distances.
- MIT OpenCourseWare 18.905 — Algebraic Topology I: singular homology, CW complexes, homological algebra, cohomology, and Poincaré duality.
- MIT OpenCourseWare 18.965 — Geometry of Manifolds: smooth manifolds, transversality, embeddings, Morse theory, differential forms, and de Rham theory.
- Harvard Mathematics 131/132/136/230A — topological spaces, fundamental groups, differential topology, manifolds, vector bundles, connections, geodesics, and curvature.
- American Mathematical Society, Beginning Topology and Topology Through Inquiry — point-set, geometric, combinatorial, differential, and algebraic topology with open-ended investigation.


## Use

Sources inform topic scope, prerequisite calibration, sequencing, and academic-level language. No source exercises, solution text, or lecture prose were copied.
