About Topology & Differential Geometry

Topology & Differential Geometry studies shape, continuity, manifolds, curvature, and global structure. Students connect abstract topological ideas with local differential methods and geometric reasoning.

Course Features

TopologyOpen sets, continuity, compactness, connectedness, quotient spaces, and homotopy develop global structural reasoning.
ManifoldsCharts, tangent spaces, smooth maps, and manifolds connect local coordinates with intrinsic geometry.
Curves & SurfacesCurvature, parametrized geometry, fundamental forms, and surface theory develop classical differential geometry.
Global GeometryDifferential forms, metrics, geodesics, curvature, and global relationships connect local computation to large-scale structure.
