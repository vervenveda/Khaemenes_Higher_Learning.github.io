Seifert–van Kampen Strategy

Accept equivalent mathematically correct reasoning. Require explicit definitions, theorem hypotheses, proof structure, verification, and limitations.

Problem 1

Begin from the exact definitions, verify all hypotheses, use π₁(U∪V) from π₁(U),π₁(V), distinguish local from global conclusions, and test the result on a standard model and a boundary or counterexample case.

Problem 2

Identify the first invalid inference, missing hypothesis, quantifier error, unjustified limit interchange, or unproved existence claim; then supply a valid repair.

Problem 3

A complete response explicitly states the definitions, domain, hypotheses, exact target, and theorem dependencies for Seifert–van Kampen Strategy.

Problem 4

The argument must address decomposing spaces into open sets and assembling fundamental groups with each nontrivial step justified and the final conclusion restated.

Problem 5

A strong application explains the mathematical model in robot motion, periodic systems, knot complements, phase spaces, and obstruction theory, verifies conditions, and names at least one limitation.

Problem 6

Accept a second valid proof, a counterexample showing necessity of a hypothesis, an independent estimate, or a reproducible finite check that is correctly described as supporting evidence.
