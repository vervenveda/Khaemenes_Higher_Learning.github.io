Simplicial Homology & Euler Characteristic

Build graphs and a filled triangle. Compute connected components and one-dimensional cycles using elementary chain-complex rank formulas. Compare a triangular loop to a triangle whose interior is filled.

Choose the simplicial complex

Chain model

Filled triangular faces become two-chains; their boundaries kill the corresponding one-dimensional homology class.

Mathematical investigation

  1. Calculate the boundary of the oriented triangle [0,1,2]: ∂₂[0,1,2]=[1,2]−[0,2]+[0,1].
  2. Verify ∂₁∂₂=0 by expanding the vertex terms.
  3. For the triangle boundary, compute dim ker ∂₁=E−V+β₀; note that no 2-simplices exist.
  4. Fill the triangle and show why im ∂₂ has rank one, reducing β₁ by one.
  5. Verify Euler–Poincaré: V−E+F=β₀−β₁+β₂ for each displayed example.

Academic review: Learners must derive the boundary maps and rank calculations; this lab supplies experimental evidence, not a substitute for proof.
