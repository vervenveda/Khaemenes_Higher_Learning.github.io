## Explicitly not misclassified as university courses

- **High-school courses** are feeder/prerequisite study, not university-degree coursework simply because adults can take them.
- **Teaching utilities, research tools and community services** (PROSE, The Verifier, PLERA Search, Arcade, Aurora, River-to-Road) can be linked later as supporting institutes, labs, and libraries; do not invent course units where no course framework is verified.
- **Internal certificates, college-style numbering or credit-equivalent hours** do not imply independent accreditation, eligibility for licensure, or transferable credit.
- The source inventory is not an exhaustive enumeration of every activity in every Verve N Veda repository; it is the first course-bearing federation pass.

## Rules for a full course-ready classification (future, not assumed)

A university course may graduate beyond this inventory only when all evidence is present:
1. Publish defined outcomes, prerequisites, syllabus, sequence, and instructional contact/independent workload.
2. Supply actual readings and worked instruction; no placeholder or generic repeated text.
3. Provide practice, discipline-appropriate laboratories or studio/research activity, and accessible accommodations.
4. Use reviewed abundant question banks, genuinely randomized **question and answer order** where relevant, clear explanations, mastery gates, and versioned saved-attempt snapshots.
5. Provide secure unseen summative examinations when promised, plus a portfolio/capstone rubric and correction/reassessment rules.
6. Preserve learner ownership with Academy identity authority; no implicit mixing across learners or silent storage migrations.
7. Pass runtime navigation, mobile, keyboard, screen-reader, printer, export/import, offline/fallback and cross-browser checks.
8. Keep transparent completion language without asserting external credit, licensure, or accredited degrees.
