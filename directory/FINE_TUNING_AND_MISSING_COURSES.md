## V. The 80% noncompensatory mastery standard

A learner may freely study and practice. In a future legally authorized assessed certificate track, they must separately pass all required outcomes and unit gates >=80%, plus required laboratory/portfolio rubrics >=80%, midterm >=80%, final >=80%, and capstone/defense >=80%. Remediation and alternate retakes remain free. **No averaging over a failed critical gate.** The real source course must be audited and instrumented for this before the candidate moves to 'academic QA complete'. A local progress widget or static GitHub Pages score does not by itself confer an institutional credential.

Review the [Mastery and Accreditation Standard](MASTERY_AND_ACCREDITATION_STANDARD.md) for technical, faculty and student safeguards.

## VI. Accreditation readiness before certificates

- Determine governing entity and actual state of operation, including exemptions or licensure prerequisites.
- Consult applicable state postsecondary regulators **before** announcing any formal postsecondary certificate program. Free tuition is a founding value, not an assumed license exemption.
- Study a recognized institutional accreditor's *scope* and requirements (e.g., DEAC for predominantly distance-based education) and prepare documented eligibility evidence.
- Establish faculty quality review, assessment security, curricular advisory committees, student services, independent governance, financial sustainability, grievance/appeal process, and durable academic records.
- Avoid unsupported degree/college-transfer/professional licensure claims.
- Release pathways one at a time only after a legal authority determination and complete academic quality review.
